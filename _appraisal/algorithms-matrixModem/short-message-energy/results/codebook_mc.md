## 1. Keyed random q-ary bank with exhaustive ML against the fully orthogonal reference

The reference energy E_ref is the exact requirement of 2^k fully orthogonal codewords with the same number
of branches n (from `short_message_energy.md`), where the message error is `target`. The bank reuses the
same q tones in every slot, so two codewords collide in a slot with probability 1/q. A fresh random
codebook is drawn every 32 trials (ensemble average).

## 2. Repetition with square-law combining: information per q-ary symbol

`I_L` is the mutual information (bits) of one q-ary symbol sent on L independently Rayleigh-faded looks at
per-look SNR g, square-law combined (ML for Rayleigh). `efficiency` = I_L / (L I_1): 1 means repetition
collects as much as an ideal lower-rate code over L distinct symbols at the same per-look SNR.
`one look` = I_1 at SNR L g: all the energy in a single coherent look (no diversity inside the symbol).

## 3. Bank decoding under interference: metrics without side information

k = 10, q = 64, random bank, exhaustive decoding with the summed per-cell metric. Both designs carry 6 dB
more energy than the no-margin requirement (E/N0 = 264): design A keeps n = 18 slots and makes each 6 dB
stronger; design B uses n = 72 slots at the no-margin slot SNR. `slot hits`: with probability rho a slot
has its noise raised to 1 + J in all q cells (pulsed or broadband interference). `cell hits`: each cell
independently has its noise raised to 1 + J with probability rho (narrowband or multitone interference
after keyed tone permutation). Entries are message error probabilities; 0 means no error in the trials.
The slot noise estimate is the median of the slot's q cell energies divided by ln 2; the `floor` is the
lower quartile of the n slot estimates of the same message. No metric except the two genie rows and the
`known quiet noise` clip uses anything but the received energies.
