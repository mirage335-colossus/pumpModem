# OTHR tolerance of the lattice receiver (0.4 s dwell)

Generated by `othr_tolerance.cpp`: 160 scenes of 96 dwells per point, test tone at -6.0 dB per dwell.
Entries: received-power margin consumed (dB) = half of the extra transmit time, relative to an
unquantized receiver without radar. `INR` is radar power while in band over noise power in 3.2 kHz.
The radar passes a steep 2.8 kHz receive filter before the modem sees it. `hot bins`: tone positions
whose noise estimate is more than twice the global one (linear chain). `notched`: tone positions that
lose more than half their energy in the comb excision. `erased`: samples erased by the q4e chain.
Simulation of a model radar; not a measurement of any specific system.
