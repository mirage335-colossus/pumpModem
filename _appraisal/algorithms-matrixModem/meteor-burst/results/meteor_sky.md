# Meteor trails over the whole shared sky: usable trails against station distance

Generated by `meteor_sky.cpp` with 8000000 sampled trails per distance. Theoretical calculation; the model and
what it leaves out are in the program header. 50 MHz, galactic noise, 0 dBi at the transmitter, the
receive gain available in every direction. A tone's worth is 12 dB of echo energy over the noise density,
a block's worth 20 dB, and 26 dB allows 6 dB for fading inside the echo.

## 1. Check against the measured long link

Spann 1987 counted about 450 trails an hour above -125 dBm with 500 W and 17 dBi beams at each end over
1,300 km. The model, with 17 dBi applied over the whole sky, gives the figure below; the real beams covered
only part of the sky, so the model should come out higher.

Model, whole sky: 1063 trails an hour above -125 dBm. Usable-orientation fraction of sampled trails: 0.1%.
