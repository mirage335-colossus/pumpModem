Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,

and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 14. One-cell tones with keyed hopping: an observer without the key

Link: one tone per 160 ms cell on 352 keyed positions, steady path, receiver coherent inside one tone,
10-bit block sized by the exact presence test of section 10e (A = 4000). FHSS hops hold `tones per hop`
tones on one of `channels` keyed hop channels; hop edges are ignored. Observer as in 11d (lattice, band,
tone and hop timing known; 0.9 detection at 1e-2 false alarm; z = 3.61), Gaussian deflection throughout:
per tone on the occupied channel d1^2 = (exp(l^2) - 1)^2 (1 - l^2) / (352 l^2) with l the observer's energy
per tone; per hop dh^2 = (tones per hop) d1^2; over the channels the per-hop likelihood is a mixture, with
deflection (exp(dh^2) - 1) / channels; the block needs hops x that = z^2. `margin` is the extra C/N0 the
observer needs. `sqrt` is the square-root ideal 5 log10(352 channels z^2 / S). `~` marks dh > 1.5 or l > 0.6,
where a sum is far from Gaussian and the figure is rough. `> x` means the observer would need tones
with l above 0.9, where this weak-tone formula stops; the margin is at least x.

Without hopping an observer at the link's own C/N0 needs about the square of the one-channel margin in
time, since weak-tone evidence grows with the square of the power.

### 14b. How short the hop has to be

In the weak-tone range the margin is proportional to dh, and dh^2 = ln(1 + x) with x = z^2 channels /
(hops per block), against x for the square-root ideal. The hopping benefit given back is therefore
5 log10(x / ln(1 + x)) dB:

| x | 0.1 | 0.3 | 1 | 3 | 10 | 30 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| given back (dB) | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.8 | 1.7 | 3.1 | 4.7 |

x = 1 is 13 hops per block for every hop channel. Longest hop that keeps x at or below 1:

| C/N0 (dB-Hz) | block | 50 channels | 1,000 channels | 10,000 channels |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| +0 | 13 min | 1.2 s | under 0.4 s | under 0.4 s |
| -10 | 18.7 h | 103 s | 5.2 s | 0.5 s |
| -20 | 77.1 d | 2.8 h | 9 min | 51 s |

What a shorter hop costs the receiver with one-cell tones: only its edges (2 x 40 ms at 0.4 s, 2 x 80 ms
above), since the receiver is coherent inside a tone and not across the hop.

| hop | 0.4 s | 4 s | 40 s | 400 s | 4000 s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| time lost to edges (dB) | 0.969 | 0.177 | 0.017 | 0.002 | 0.000 |
