## 19. Ten-bit and one-bit presence at selectable null-block budgets

One public block has A nuisance hypotheses per payload. Each template receives the same
null budget alpha / (A × 2^k). The union bound allows dependent templates with valid marginal
tails. The required miss probability on the true template stays at 0.001.

S is the weak-tone Gaussian deflection. E1, E32 and E1024 are steady-path E/N0 from the
chi-square model for the indicated count of independent, equal-noise complex looks. Each
ratio divides the ten-bit requirement by the one-bit requirement at the same alpha.
Each saving compares ten bits at the listed alpha with ten bits at 1e-6.

### Ten bits at a relaxed budget against one bit at 1e-6

Ratios divide the ten-bit requirement at the listed alpha by the original one-bit requirement
at 1e-6. Both modes search the same A and keep the true-template miss probability at 0.001.

| A | ten-bit alpha | S ratio | E1 ratio | E32 ratio | E1024 ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 1 | 1e-03 | 0.967 | 0.970 | 0.978 | 0.982 |
| 400 | 1e-04 | 1.059 | 1.054 | 1.039 | 1.031 |
| 400 | 1e-03 | 0.976 | 0.978 | 0.984 | 0.987 |
| 4000 | 1e-04 | 1.053 | 1.050 | 1.036 | 1.029 |
| 4000 | 1e-03 | 0.978 | 0.980 | 0.985 | 0.988 |

All four ratios equal 1 at ten-bit alpha = (1024/2) × 1e-6 = 5.12e-4.
At that budget the two modes allocate exactly the same null probability to each template.
Above it the ten-bit presence test needs less signal under these assumptions.

At fixed received power and duty factor, a ratio of E gives the active-time and wall-time ratio
if the same count of steady looks can scale in duration. At fixed duration and look count, its
power saving in dB is 10 log10(E_old/E_new). At fixed per-look weak signal and geometry, S
scales with the number of looks and unrounded duration. At fixed duration, weak-tone deflection
scales with squared power, giving a power saving of 5 log10(S_old/S_new). A fixed transmitted
block keeps its scheduled duration when a receiver alone changes its threshold. Discrete dwell
floors, fading, wrong active-payload winners and operator recognition require separate checks.
