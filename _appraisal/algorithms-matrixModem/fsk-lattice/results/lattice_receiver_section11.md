# Matrix Modem FSK lattice: receiver calculations

Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,
B 4 s / 24 cells, C 40 s / 249 cells. Phase and fading are independent from dwell to dwell. Known timing
and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 11. FHSS dwell length when every dwell has a new phase and frequency offset

With FHSS the receiver can be coherent only inside a dwell. Phase is taken as independent from dwell to
dwell throughout. A dwell period D has D/0.16 - 1 flat cells (2 cells for 0.4 s) between its edges.

### 11c. Steady path: energy of a 10-bit block of n dwells, each with its own phase and centre

Constant-amplitude tone, a new phase in every dwell, and in every dwell a centre that is one of K cells
(K = 1: centre known). Per-dwell statistic: ln of the mean over the K cells of exp(a x)/(1 + E_d), with
a = E_d/(1 + E_d); the block sums it. Threshold tau = ln(A 2^k / 1e-6) = 29.0 with A = 4000, which bounds
the false-presence rate for any likelihood ratio; miss rate 1e-3 by Monte Carlo (20000 blocks per point).
`exact` is the chi-square calculation of section 10e for K = 1, as a check of the method.

### 11d. An observer without the key

The link is the K = 1 block of 11c with n dwells (exact energies). The observer knows the lattice, the
band and the dwell timing but not the key, so in each dwell the tone is in one of N cells (tone positions
times hop channels). Its statistic is the same per-dwell log of the mean over N cells, summed over the
block. `margin` is how much more C/N0 than the link the observer needs to detect the block with
probability 0.9 at a false-alarm rate of 1e-2 (threshold from noise-only blocks; 20,000 resampled blocks
per point). A wideband radiometer over W for the block time T with a perfectly known noise level needs
C/N0 = z sqrt(W/T) with z = 3.61; its margin is 10 log10(z sqrt(W T) / E_block).
