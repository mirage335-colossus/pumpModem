# Matrix Modem FSK lattice: receiver calculations

Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,
B 4 s / 24 cells, C 40 s / 249 cells. Phase and fading are independent from dwell to dwell. Known timing
and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 10. FHSS off: contiguous dwells and coherent accumulation across dwells

Without FHSS the carrier phase does not change between dwells and no quiet interval is needed. The dwell
is then a whole number of cells, back to back, and every tone on the lattice has phase zero at every dwell
boundary, so the transmitted phase is continuous and known.

### 10b. Coherent accumulation over W dwells (large alphabet, exact)

The receiver removes the known transmit phase and treats W consecutive 0.32 s dwells as one observation,
with spread-matched weights. This needs the channel phase to be common to the tone positions used (a
narrow total band on one propagation mode) and the frequency known to a fraction of 1/(W x 0.32 s).
Gap to the bound (dB of time); W = 1 is the per-dwell receiver.

### 10d. Few tone positions: the keyed block decoded coherently (Monte Carlo)

The format of the specification with k = 10 bits: 32 symbols of 256 values, value = G_j c xor m_j, and 8
dwells per symbol (256 dwells). Each dwell maps the 256 values onto M tone positions by a fresh keyed
balanced map (256/M values per position; one-to-one for M = 256). Phase-stable path, one unknown phase for
the block, Gaussian noise. The receiver sums complex bin values into one 256-entry table per symbol and
takes the largest |sum over symbols|^2 over the 1024 messages. E/N0 is the energy of the whole block.
Reference: 1024 exactly orthogonal codewords (numerical integral). Predicted loss: -10 log10(1 - 1/M).
20000 blocks per M; the same noise is reused across E/N0.

### 10e. Block duration estimate on a phase-stable path, FHSS off

The block is accepted by a presence test on the best message: the sum over coherent windows of the
squared magnitude of each window's sum. Threshold for a false-presence rate of 1e-6 over A 2^k tests with
A = 4000 (ten times the hypotheses of section 7, for the window bank and its finer frequency grid); miss
rate 1e-3. Exact central and noncentral chi-square tails up to 20,000 windows, Gaussian above. The energy
is reduced by (1 - 1/M) for M = 16 tone positions (section 10d). Known frequency and timing, Gaussian
noise, the path's phase steady over each window. Estimate only: no coded simulation, no margin.
