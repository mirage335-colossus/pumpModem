## 21. Steady-path block periods at the attended target

One joint quiet-block budget of 1e-4 reserves 5e-5 for each of the coherent and per-tone searches.
Each family in this example tests at most 4,000 nuisance hypotheses per word in total, including its
rungs and offsets, across 1,024 words. The Bonferroni allowance per template is 1.220703125e-11.
Miss probability on the true template is 0.001. Noise is independent complex Gaussian across
disjoint windows. Timing, frequency, noise scale and phase law within each window match the template.
These are theoretical block periods, excluding two trailing quiet blocks and all acquisition or decoding errors.

### HF held tone, FHSS on

One tone per hop. The active aperture is 2, 24, 249, 2,499 or 24,999 cells of 0.16 s respectively.
Only active time contributes signal energy. Retunes allow independent unknown phases, so the
per-tone receiver adds energies. At least 32 hops, with exactly 32L transmitted tones.

### Sub-9 kHz, FHSS off

Sixteen positions, 10.24 s tones. Subtracting the mean of 16 orthogonal position bins
in the ideal projection retains 15/16 of the energy. The block uses 32L tones with L >= 2.
Each window is normalized for its actual noise variance, including any short final window.
The noncentrality uses only transmitted energy. Maximum window lengths are one tone, 128 tones
(1,310.72 s), 2,048 tones (20,971.52 s), or the whole block. The two longer fixed windows
assume that their boundaries match the modeled changes of phase.

All table entries are elapsed seconds. The two budgeted families together still require joint
testing on actual scores, interference and wrong-schedule traffic. A successful threshold
crossing can return a competing word. In the ideal model the sum of normalized energies has
a central or noncentral chi-square law after multiplying by two. The calculation evaluates
its tails through Poisson probabilities for every row, including large window counts.
For the 128- and 2,048-tone caps it checks every shorter full-window endpoint and solves
inside the first eligible window count. The single-tone and hop columns use a bracketed
search over complete 32-tone increments. They report feasible periods. Where the format floor
does not determine the answer, the preceding legal length has miss probability above 0.001.
