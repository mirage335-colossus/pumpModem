# Matrix Modem FSK lattice: receiver calculations

Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,
B 4 s / 24 cells, C 40 s / 249 cells. Phase and fading are independent from dwell to dwell. Known timing
and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 8. Reduced transforms for the 0.4 s dwell

What a cheaper transform costs in sensitivity. `all bins` weights every 3.125 Hz fine bin (section 2).
`tone grid` computes only the 6.25 Hz tone-position bins and weights those (half the bins: the case in
which a matrix-product DFT is faster than an FFT). `one bin` uses the tone's own bin alone. Large
alphabet, exact. First the gap to the bound (dB of time), then the loss against `all bins` as
received power (half the time difference at weak signals, the full difference is printed for +10 dB-Hz).
