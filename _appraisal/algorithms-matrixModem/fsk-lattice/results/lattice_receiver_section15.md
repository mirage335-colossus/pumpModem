Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,

and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 15. Ten bits against one bit at the same confidence

A block of k bits is sized by its presence test: false-presence rate 1e-6 over A 2^k tests, miss rate
1e-3. `S` is the Gaussian deflection (z_fa + z_miss)^2 of section 7, to which block time is proportional
for weak tones. `E` is the exact total E/N0 (chi-square tails, steady path) for a block added as n
energies. Ratios are against k = 1 in the same row group; at fixed power they are ratios of transmit time.
