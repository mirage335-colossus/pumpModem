# Matrix Modem FSK lattice: receiver calculations

Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,
B 4 s / 24 cells, C 40 s / 249 cells. Phase and fading are independent from dwell to dwell. Known timing
and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 16. Losing hops: how many hops a block has to span

Each hop is unusable with probability p, independently (interference on some hop frequencies or during
some hops), and the receiver treats an unusable hop as erased. A margin of M dB tolerates the loss of a
fraction f of the block's tones (0.57, 0.77, 0.87, 0.90, 0.91, 0.92 for 3, 6, 10, 13, 16, 20 dB, from
../short-message-energy). The block's tones are spread evenly over H hops. Smallest H for which more than
f of the hops are lost with probability at most 1e-3 and 1e-2 (exact binomial); `-` if p is not below f.

**Probability at most 0.001**

| p lost | 3 dB | 6 dB | 10 dB | 13 dB | 16 dB | 20 dB |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.25 | 22 | 10 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 0.50 | 492 | 33 | 18 | 14 | 14 | 14 |
| 0.75 | - | 4436 | 117 | 72 | 60 | 54 |

**Probability at most 0.01**

| p lost | 3 dB | 6 dB | 10 dB | 13 dB | 16 dB | 20 dB |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.25 | 15 | 6 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 0.50 | 285 | 19 | 11 | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| 0.75 | - | 2536 | 65 | 43 | 37 | 31 |

Losing a fraction p of the hops costs 1/(1 - p) in usable time: 3.0 dB of time for half the hops, which
is about 1.5 dB of power for weak tones. That is the sense in which lost hops act as a higher noise floor.
