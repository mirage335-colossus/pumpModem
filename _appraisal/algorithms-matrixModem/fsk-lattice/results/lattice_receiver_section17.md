# Matrix Modem FSK lattice: receiver calculations

Generated by `lattice_receiver.cpp`. Complex baseband at 3200 Hz, cell = 512 samples = 160 ms, bin = 6.25 Hz.
Dwell period D with nc whole active cells starting 40 ms after the dwell start: A 0.4 s / 2 cells,
B 4 s / 24 cells, C 40 s / 249 cells. Phase and fading are independent from dwell to dwell. Known timing
and Doppler centre, Gaussian noise, no interference, ideal coding. Theoretical calculation.

## 17. Tones without a keyed phase: the cell-rate pulse an observer can add coherently

Every position completes whole cycles in a cell. If every tone starts its cell at the same phase, the mean
of the signal over the P unknown positions is (1/P) sum_k exp(j 2 pi k n / N): a pulse at each cell start
holding 1/P of the tone energy. An observer correlates each cell with that pulse and adds the M results
coherently, with no knowledge of the positions. With l the energy per tone over the noise density, the sum
is one complex value of mean-square M l / P over unit noise; one carrier phase is unknown, so the test is on
its magnitude (exact, noncentral chi-square with one complex term). Steady phase at the observer over the
whole observation; cell timing known; P = 352. The energy observer is that of section 14 with one hop
channel. Detection 0.9 at 1e-2 false alarm, shared over the frequency bins searched. Theoretical.

### 17c. Monte Carlo in the time domain: no key, two-phase key, four-phase key
