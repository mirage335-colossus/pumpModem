## 3. Quantizing the per-dwell evidence

One-bin evidence z (noise mean 1, exponential law) quantized to `g = min(2^b - 1, floor(z / step))`, the
integer g then being summed over dwells. `low-SNR loss` is `-10 log10 Corr0(g, z)^2`: the extra transmit
time relative to summing unquantized energies (the power-equivalent is half). Closed form. The step is
optimized for low SNR; `clip` is the noise-only probability of the top level. The other columns are the
large-alphabet GMI loss (dB of time) in Rayleigh fading at the stated mean SNR per dwell with the same step.

## 6. Unknown Doppler shift of the path

Sections 2 to 5 assume the Doppler centre is known. Here the true centre lies anywhere within +-1 Hz
(mid-latitude shifts are of that order and wander over tens of minutes). `known` repeats section 2.
`wide` uses one fixed weighting matched to the channel smeared over +-1 Hz (no tracking). `bank` tests
centre hypotheses one fine bin apart and uses the nearest, which needs the shift to hold still over the
evidence that selects it; for the 0.4 s dwell one fine bin is wider than the range, so `bank` is centred weights. Large alphabet, C/N0 = -10 dB-Hz; extra transmit time (dB) relative to the bound.

## 7. Block duration estimate

A block of k bits is accepted when its best codeword exceeds a presence threshold. With accumulated
evidence that is close to Gaussian, the block needs a total deflection S = (z_fa + z_miss)^2, where
z_fa = Qinv(P_fa / (A 2^k)) for A timing and frequency hypotheses and z_miss = Qinv(P_miss). The dwell
count is S / d2 with d2 = 2 GMI (nat per dwell) of the spread-matched receiver of section 2, and never
fewer than 32 dwells (the diversity a short block needs in fading). P_fa = 1e-6 per block, P_miss = 1e-3.
