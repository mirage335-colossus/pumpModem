## 2. Simple receivers: FT8-like single bin, multi-bin segments, fully coherent

the best Doppler offset (as a receiver locking to the strongest mode would). `best INC` is
optimized over Ts ∈ {5 ms … 41 s} and B ∈ {0,1,2,4,8}. `COH best` optimizes the dwell of a single bin.

frequency offset −(B_c/T)·τ. Equal-power paths, each Gaussian with the stated 2σ. The tone sees the
sum of the two paths as one process (delays only rotate phase). Both receivers are matched weighted
periodograms over the dwell T; entries are the gap (dB) to the flat-fading bound for the same spectrum.
