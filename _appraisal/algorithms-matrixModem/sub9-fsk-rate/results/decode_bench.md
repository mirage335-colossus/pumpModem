# Block decode cost on a CPU: per-tone, full window ladder and table ladder

Generated by `decode_bench.cpp` with 12 threads. One decode scores all 2^k messages of one hypothesis.
Every measurement keeps all threads busy with tasks of one (repetition, high half) and does at least
1.5e+10 lookups; small blocks are repeated, large ones are timed on a sample of high halves and scaled
(`timed` gives the number of decodes actually done). Synthetic 4-bit tables with a planted message.
