## 1. Bandwidth that 10 kW buys at the voltage limit

At a top voltage V the current is I = V w C and the radiated power (V w C)^2 Rr, whatever the loss. A
transmitter with 10 kW to spend can afford a series resistance R = P / I^2, which gives the -3 dB
bandwidth B* = P / (2 pi C V^2). It does not depend on frequency. Below B* the 10 kW cannot be used;
above it the radiated power falls as 1/B.
