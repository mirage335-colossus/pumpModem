7. Purpose, team scope and ambition — project owner’s supplied context
Data Pump’s main product purpose is safe data transfer. Weak-link/radio capability
is a useful extension. The broader impetus was a demanding example to exercise
development practices, as a starting point for Software Foundation. The film says
“developers from the team” and “one example of the team’s capabilities”; it makes
no claim that this project represents their full abilities.
The owner estimates the starting project at approximately two weeks. The few-hour,
potentially one-hour outcome is narrated and labelled as an ambition for comparable
development breadth. No universal completeness or measured speedup is asserted.

9. Historical context, revision3
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/w1hkj/fldigi/master/src/include/jalocha/pj_mfsk.h
The creator’s Olivia modem source dates the design to December2004.
https://www.w1hkj.org/FldigiHelp/olivia_page.html
Selected common Olivia configurations in the official manual deliver approximately
15–29 words/minute. This supports Morse-like text speed for these configurations.
https://www.arrl.org/code-transmissions
ARRL fast Morse practice uses10–35 words/minute. This is a concrete pace comparison,
not a claim that all operators or all Olivia modes run at those speeds.
The video first presents these common configurations, then explicitly changes to
the faster4/2000~109bit/s variant for the260x rate comparison. They are not conflated.
A blanket claim that no comparable radio-modem leap occurred for decades is not
established: other high-rate PSK/MFSK and PACTOR modes exist with differing channel
assumptions. The film states Olivia’s age and the measured comparison precisely.

https://www.scs-pactor.com/pactor-4
