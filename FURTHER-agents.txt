=========================================================

the matching project source, retained SDK/dependency archives and explicitly
documented distro packages already available locally. Require no internet,
third-party Git checkout, undeclared cache or supplier download during SDK
restoration, application compilation or packaging. Keep build-host compatibility
separate from the portable binaries' runtime compatibility.

SDK only for x86_64. ARM64 builds instead use Ubuntu 22.04 and online preparation
of CMake, Clang from apt.llvm.org, Ninja and SDL. ARM64 portable binaries pass
Bookworm runtime checks, but that does not qualify an ARM64 offline source build.
The independent September 30 Bookworm recovery record covers x86_64, not ARM64.
