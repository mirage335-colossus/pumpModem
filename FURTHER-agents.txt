2026-10-13 / Codex side conversation: finish practical Sched_Hop acceleration
=============================================================================

Continue the current Sched_Hop work, preserving all existing corrections,
runnable references, investigations, measurements and other agents' work.

The central acceptance criterion needs particular attention:

Sched_Hop must make an existing, useful, extensively optimized
continuous-reception configuration practical when that receiver is slightly
too slow for real time. It should retain the useful decoder and most of its
existing acceleration, while supplying additional physical time to process the
same source waveform.

This does not require extending the continuous receiver into previously
unsupported weak-signal territory. Hold the configuration and target SNR fixed
when establishing the scheduling benefit.

Keep everything local. Follow AGENTS.md, coordination and the development
contract. Reuse completed work. Serialize heavy measurements. Do not push,
publish, dispatch hosted CI, add GPU work or start hours-long platform/SDK
qualification. Deliver a rebuilt GUI at a manual-testing checkpoint.

1. Establish the correct comparisons.

Compare:
- The latest optimized continuous receiver with Sched_Hop disabled.
- The preceding scheduled implementation.
- The corrected scheduled implementation.

Record the actual executed decoder, algorithms, arithmetic, reuse and fallback
coverage.

Use identical original PCM for receiver-only changes. Enabling scheduling
changes the capture: compare those waveforms using matched synthetic keys,
payloads, channel/noise conditions and explicit active-power/energy
constraints.

Do not count an improvement between two scheduled implementations as proof
that scheduling preserves continuous-receiver efficiency.

Measure aggregate CPU and elapsed processing separately. Keep input rates,
available workers and hardware conditions matched; report
application-selected-rate comparisons separately.

2. Audit scheduling-induced loss of acceleration.

Identify every condition under which enabling Sched_Hop disables or bypasses
an existing FFT, frequency-axis transform, pulse projection, template reuse,
reduced-precision path or batching mechanism.

For each restriction, distinguish:
- An inherent requirement arising from physical capture time or uncertainty.
- A restriction of the current representation or implementation.
- An unsupported numerical approximation.
- A measured algorithm crossover.

An eligibility exclusion is not an impossibility proof. An unsuccessful large
sparse FFT, correlation-only transform, cache implementation or arithmetic
microprototype does not rule out the broader strategy.

Prioritize restoring efficient representations across split symbols. Do not
spend the remainder solely optimizing already-expensive direct candidate loops
or introducing more caches.

3. Implement practical calendar-aware acceleration.

Investigate and implement the strongest applicable combination of:
- Bounded tiled or partitioned correlation over active intervals.
- Shared pulse projections and real I/Q sufficient statistics.
- Integrated energy, Gram and guard processing.
- Frequency-family transforms or fusion.
- Candidate-family batching and cross-bank preprocessing reuse.
- Productive native arithmetic under the existing Search arithmetic policy.

Include preparation, packing, plans, allocation, cache misses,
synchronization, numerical controls, refinement and publication in complete
cost.

Preserve physical carrier time across gaps. Source coordinates pause; physical
phase does not. Never concatenate separated bursts as adjacent physical
samples, count inserted zeros as observations, or allocate enormous
mostly-null transforms.

Some additional frequency/clock coverage may be mathematically necessary.
Reduce its execution cost rather than dropping required hypotheses or assuming
every hypothesis needs independent sample-by-sample reconstruction.

4. Explicitly search for consequential scheduling-induced cliffs.

Compare scheduling off/on across nearby configurations, including:
- Small changes in symbol sample count, chip duration and sample rate.
- Integer and fractional boundaries and partial final chips.
- Default timing and genuinely qualified tighter timing.
- DSSS Off/10/100/1000 and FHSS off/on where the continuous configuration is
  supported.
- Multiplier and dilution changes.
- Broader oscillator uncertainty.
- Multiple keys, rolling admissions and constrained workspace.
- Default, FP32 minimum and FP64 force.

Inspect GCD/canonical-phase changes, timing subdivisions, backend eligibility
thresholds, frequency/clock caps, per-bank workspace shares, cache exhaustion
and fallback storms.

Distinguish cliffs already present in continuous reception from additional
costs introduced by scheduling. Report actual combined arrival uncertainty;
capture timestamp radius alone is not the arrival window.

A broader weakening sweep can diagnose scaling, but do not demand scheduling
support for configurations the continuous receiver itself cannot use.

5. Demonstrate the intended processing headroom.

Predeclare at least one useful configuration that accumulates backlog under
independent continuous input because processing is slightly too slow.

With scheduling enabled, demonstrate:
- The preferred useful complex strategy remains available.
- Processing can span multiple cycles.
- Average throughput is sustainable with meaningful margin.
- Backlog and memory remain bounded and the capture drains completely.
- Accepted bits publish promptly and physical completion remains correct.

Measure CPU per active/source second and per physical second, elapsed
throughput, queue age, peak backlog, memory and publication latency.

Do not let producer backpressure hide an unsustainable receiver. Do not impose
an unnecessary per-hop clearance deadline.

Greater dilution is useful to investigate, but a specially tuned case must not
conceal failures in ordinary scheduling settings.

6. Preserve correctness and useful sensitivity.

Preserve transmitted bits, framing, independent private candidates,
cryptographic addressing, all admitted hypotheses, real I/Q, template/noise
energy, covariance, trial accounting and interference guards.

Preserve cancellation, pending identity and observed-absence completion.
Nulls, EOF, flushing and queue retirement must not manufacture absence.

FP64 force remains the original-template diagnostic reference. Default may use
existing authorized approximations with appropriate numerical and sensitivity
evidence; do not impose accidental bitwise-reference requirements that
prohibit legitimate acceleration.

Validate receiver-only statistic/decision agreement, threshold-region behavior
and important boundaries. Clearly distinguish measured additional loss,
uncertainty and unqualified scope.

7. Complete the implementation assessment honestly.

For each substantial applicable strategy, provide:
- Implemented and measured.
- Prototyped and rejected with complete execution evidence.
- Quantitatively bounded out for a precise scope.
- Outstanding with a concrete blocker.

Do not claim unavoidable cliffs without evidence that distinguishes
mathematical necessity from the current implementation.

Do not declare the implementation objective complete while major applicable
acceleration strategies remain unattempted without a defensible bound or
blocker.

Run focused checks, then complete affected local groups. Rebuild the GUI and
provide normal and verbose/debug launchers.

Report the scheduling overhead relative to the optimized continuous receiver,
demonstrated rescued configurations, cliff/boundary coverage, remaining
restrictions and deferred qualification.

An honest partial checkpoint is permissible, but must explicitly state that
Sched_Hop's practical processing-headroom objective remains unfinished if the
matched sustained-reception demonstration has not succeeded.
