# Historical experiment integrity record

This page is a record of the completed archive audit, not instructions to retain, transfer, extract, or reproduce the campaign package. The [extension report](../README.md), [physical results](../physical/README.md), and [phase-continuity results](../phase/README.md) preserve the conclusions and limits. Future intensive testing follows the maintained [abbreviated triad harness](../../../../triad-performance.md), [Robust testing policy](../../../../robust-current.md#testing-computationally-intensive-robust-changes), and [material lifecycle policy](../../../../development.md#material-lifecycle).

The historical archive was reported as 7,168,684,665 compressed bytes with SHA-256 `fcedba8e0c40d48a3e06fed83250552619f8e992f125353c850d092de41702cf`, containing 8,635 files and 42,940,313,616 logical payload bytes (28,532,930,047 unique regular bytes). A local integrity pass checked the archive, inventory, files and hardlink targets without extracting payloads. The prior cloud packer matched 4,952 output bindings against 56 stage receipts; the local integrity pass did not repeat that semantic audit.

The bundle represented original and repaired scoring outputs, failures, sampled workspaces, binaries, build/source material and campaign diagnostics. Its distinct source versions are provenance for different historical runs, not competing implementations to keep current. Integrity verification does not make the archive a test dependency or qualify the campaign scientifically. Preserve only evidence required by a concrete unresolved question and remove temporary copies once that question is resolved.
