# Candidate receiver architecture

Research design, November 2026. The comparison starts with one fixed waveform and one detection problem. It changes the arithmetic and factorization before asking whether a different waveform is worth testing. None of the routes below has a measured Matrix Modem sensitivity or service rate.

## Waveform and receiver boundary

- **Transmitter.** Start with one constant-envelope tone at a time, with continuous phase where practical and a reproducible transition/taper at the occupied-band edges. Use publicly configured slot geometry. The schedule selects a group of legal tones, a permutation from bits to tone labels, and optionally a hop across the usable passband. One schedule instance is fixed before the transmission begins. Compare a fixed frequency and hopping at equal energy and completion deadline. A hop permutation makes adjacent labels hard to predict with an unknown key, while its diversity comes from the actual frequency separation and channel correlation.
- **Duration and spacing.** Use 160 ms, or 6.25 symbols/s, as a reference, with 80 and 40 ms *receiver apertures within the same transmitted symbol* as alternatives. Use 12.5 and 18.75 Hz tone spacing as first stress-screen settings, and include the ideal rectangular 6.25 Hz spacing as a control. A taper, settling time and delay guard change the effective correlation aperture and cross-label leakage. Test a binary control and a 16-ary option. The latter carries at most 25 uncoded label bits/s at 6.25 symbols/s before redundancy, gaps or errors. A shorter transmitted symbol is a subsequent matched-airtime experiment, with its own spectrum and acquisition tests. Longer tones remain a candidate on measured coherent paths. [Waveform comparison](waveform-options.md).
- **Front end.** View the 450–2650 Hz application passband as a starting geometry for an SSB chain; move its center to complex baseband and include the real equipment/filter response. In one example, sample at 3,200 complex samples/s after anti-alias filtering, with a 2,200 Hz usable band and 500 Hz transition space per edge. Quantize both components at the declared boundary, with a fixed mapping or explicitly bounded causal gain/scale selection. Log clipping, front-end invalidity, filter memory and usable bandwidth separately. A valid quiet interval requires coverage of the complete expected DATA aperture and its physical tail. [HF channels](hf-channels.md), [spectrum and stream contract](stream-keys-and-spectrum.md).
- **Helper.** Accept a causal estimate and uncertainty for time, frequency and clock stretch from Helper or a calibrated external reference. Apply a needed front-end correction before the DATA quantizer, or account for an explicitly admissible post-boundary implementation. A common correction removes common drift; residual per-path spread, delay, and uncertainty remain in the DATA search. Keep Helper support and power in the end-to-end ledger. Include Helper off, successful assistance, unsuccessful acquisition, and wrong or stale guidance. A failed or absent Helper estimate cannot supply quiet evidence. Optional pilot observations never enter the DATA likelihood as additional observations.
- **Arithmetic.** Keep the post-boundary samples and reference coefficients in signed INT4. Keep packed bit patterns, key processing and array addresses distinct from numerical signal precision. The strict route constructs scores with small-radix digits and explicit carry/overflow semantics. The direct hardware route uses signed four-bit multiplication with a hardware-required signed 32-bit accumulator, a distinct conditional experiment that requires resolving the precision constraint. The hardware route cannot be the strict baseline. [Arithmetic and accelerator routes](int4-and-accelerators.md).

The TX sequence can be generated in advance in independent bounded tiles after assigning absolute slots and sample indices. For phase continuity, form per-tile phase increments, perform a prefix calculation over those initial phases, and render disjoint tiles from the resulting state. Keep filter convolving history across tiles. A same-stream simulation similarly makes reproducible noise and fading innovations with coordinate-addressed seeds and renders stateless portions in parallel. Correlated path states, oscillator drift, delayed tails and filter overlap must survive tile boundaries. Construct ramped, overlapping, out-of-range and muted intervals as physical effects with distinct validity and expected quiet labels. Use the actual native generator and receive path in the simulator. A reduced model that draws projections analytically can screen mathematical choices, with no claim about sampled front-end, quantizer, channel evolution or throughput.

## What the matrix computes

For window `b`, form complex samples `x_b[n]=r_b[n]+i q_b[n]`. Let a fixed candidate column `h` contain `c_h[n]+i s_h[n]`, including the expected tone, window and any fixed subspace basis. The reusable primitive is the complex inner product

`z[b,h] = Σ_n x_b[n] conj(h[n])`.

For a real packed matrix, place `[r[0:N], q[0:N]]` in row `b`. Column `2h` of the template matrix is `[c, s]`, and column `2h+1` is `[-s, c]`. This produces `Re(z), Im(z)` in one real product with shape `[B,2N] × [2N,2F]`. Generate both signs from the same symmetric codebook, such as −7 to 7, so negation never attempts to represent +8. Record the actual discrete vector norm and cross-correlations, including after taper and I/Q imbalance. Complex components in the input can still use −8 if the sample mapping calls for it. An all-zero row is not evidence of quiet when the input is invalid or the device failed.

For a single ideal unit-norm template under white complex Gaussian noise and exact timing/frequency, `v=|z|²/ν` is exponential with unit mean under `H0`, where `ν` denotes the noise variance *of the projection*. With unknown deterministic complex amplitude under `H1`, the GLRT is monotone in `v`. If the amplitude is modeled as circular Gaussian with projected signal-to-noise ratio `ρ`, `v` has mean `1+ρ`, and the log likelihood ratio is `ρ v/(1+ρ) − log(1+ρ)`. The constant matters when comparing different support, normalization, or aperture count. With independent `H0` coordinates and an `H1` whitened covariance increment having eigenvalues `λ_j`, the corresponding statistic is

`L = Σ_j [(λ_j/(1+λ_j)) |z_j|² − log(1+λ_j)]`, with whitened projections `z_j`.

Jointly whiten or explicitly account for the Gram/covariance of overlapping, tapered, nonorthogonal coordinates. For disjoint apertures with independent noise and independent path gains, the spectrum and weights can reduce to independent scalar terms. A few fixed channel components can share projection tiles; model an uncertain path as a low-rank alternative before testing a large explicit drift/trajectory bank. A covariance mismatch can damage power or calibration. In particular, extra candidate modes raise the noise burden when they carry little wanted energy. The ideal Gaussian expressions are analysis and calibration references. Code-domain threshold tables or limb arithmetic should implement a specified approximation whose whole-search null and wanted behavior are tested. [Scharf and Friedlander, matched subspaces](https://doi.org/10.1109/78.301849), [Zhu et al., few-bit line detection, author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00491).

### Acquisition and label scoring

1. Form common grids of window starts and absolute frequency coordinates, using stable configured bounds for oscillator offset, sample-clock slope, time uncertainty and residual propagation. The nominal hop/label map is a gather from this physical bank. Multiple public clock/ordinal alternatives often refer to the same projection even when their schedules differ. Identical projections can be shared with their separate public alternative identities and likelihood weights intact. Use accumulated label likelihoods across an epoch candidate only with a model that accounts for their dependence. Prefer running coarse and fine shapes, with separately accounted cells, to a huge per-trajectory template. Baseline an aligned calendar with bounded offset. Blind epoch/ordinal acquisition requires its own observability and cost study.
2. For each candidate timing/offset and each possible DATA label, collect expected tone evidence and compare with a physical noise/interference model `H0`. Also compare leading label likelihoods and the expected off-tone controls. Off-tone data can estimate or test the background when frequency separation and filter leakage permit, with covariance and contamination tested on independent captures. Do not pair a tested tone with a control containing probable wanted smear, occupied neighbors, or the same adaptive peak selection. A noise-only null, an unrelated tone and a wrong-key transmission are different populations. Strong wrong-key or structured interference can leave ambiguous DATA or quiet even at large signal power. [Prior normalized-null counterexample](../../../_appraisal/highFidelity-triple_Robust_Modem/hf-normalized-null.md), [prior predictive quiet analysis](../../../_appraisal/highFidelity-triple_Robust_Modem/hf-predictive-completion.md).
3. A calibrated detection score distinguishes signal from null; a margin or soft vector distinguishes labels and can provide erasures for short fixed code groups. Choose either a maximum with a search-wide threshold or a mixture with fixed prior weights, and test it on the full candidate process including coarse-to-fine selection. Selecting and renormalizing weights on the same data changes the statistic. Compare a promising prior against held-out realizations that do not share the receiver template bank. Coding/interleaving latency and early false end remain visible in the comparison. [Recent finite-blocklength joint synchronization study, model-specific preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41580v1).
4. Chronological state commits `DATA`, scored physical absence, or an unresolved/invalid observation. Interior losses remain erasures if activity resumes. A per-symbol non-detection is weak absence evidence in a deep fade. Base completion on a declared run of independently supported quiet observations, accounting for correlation in its calibration and keeping known occupied, clipped, unobserved, resource-limited and ambiguous intervals out of the quiet class. Consecutive quiet intervals qualify no guarantee through a whole outage; report false end and censored/incomplete outcomes. The maximum local storage or time quota can stop work with an incomplete outcome. Helper, ciphertext, received lengths, software progress and simulated transmitter metadata do not decide physical completion. The simulator truth labels outcomes after scoring. [Existing physical completion evidence](../../../docs/robust-manual-capacity.md#physical-completion-and-security).

### Time–frequency checks before an implementation choice

For one rectangular tone and a deterministic residual offset `δf`, power efficiency is `sinc²(δf T)`, with `sinc(u)=sin(πu)/(πu)`. At a half-bin residual (`|δf|=0.5/T`) the loss is 3.92 dB. Grid spacing `0.5/T` bounds the residual to `0.25/T`, for at most 0.91 dB in this geometry. At 160 ms that spacing is 3.125 Hz. A 1.5625 Hz grid is a finer independent test of detector and bank cost. Neither grid compensates for stochastic variation. A pure rectangular tone has null-to-null main-lobe width `2/T`. Its taper, transitions, Doppler-cloud overlap and occupied emission must be evaluated independently of tone spacing.

For a centered stationary Gaussian *Doppler power spectrum* with standard deviation `σ_f`, the mean fraction of energy captured by the one whole-aperture constant-frequency projection is

`η(T) = 2/T² ∫₀ᵀ (T−u) exp(−2π² σ_f² u²) du`.

With `a=√2 π σ_f T`, this is `√π erf(a)/a + (exp(−a²)−1)/a²`. The calculation uses one correctly centered stochastic tap and compares expected projection energy with `C T`; it excludes multipath timing error, inter-tone leakage, search and variances. The full `2σ_f` convention follows F.1487. [ITU-R F.1487](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-F.1487/en).

| Full `2σ_f` width | 160 ms `10 log10 η` | 80 ms | 40 ms |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.5 Hz | −0.02 dB | −0.01 dB | ≈0 dB |
| 2 Hz | −0.35 dB | −0.09 dB | −0.02 dB |
| 6.25 Hz | −2.25 dB | −0.78 dB | −0.22 dB |
| 10 Hz | −3.77 dB | −1.66 dB | −0.52 dB |
| 30 Hz | −8.03 dB | −5.27 dB | −2.81 dB |

Shortening the window reduces this coherence loss and the energy in *each* look. At `C/N₀=0 dB-Hz`, the full constant-gain `E_s/N₀` before this loss is −7.96 dB at 160 ms and −13.98 dB at 40 ms. For `L` disjoint looks with independent unit-exponential noise energies, summing type-matched energy has `H0` standard deviation `√L`; its low-signal mean shift is `(C/N₀) T_total η(T_look)` for a single aperture shape. Its deflection relative to one long look is therefore `η(T_look)/(√L η(T_total))`. Four 40 ms looks improve that narrow metric by about 2.2 dB when expressed as equivalent required `C/N₀` at 30 Hz width (`10 log10` of the ratio), and worsen it by about 3.0 dB at 0.5 Hz. Correlated fading and multiple tested modes affect tail probabilities and diversity. This is a conditional motivation to test segmented, whole-window and low-rank scoring, with no predicted decoding gain or optimal aperture. A receiver that can preserve justified inter-aperture phase has another comparison to beat. [Information limits](information-limits.md).

Frequency diversity needs a separate measurement. For two equal-power independent circular-Gaussian taps at delays 0 and `τ`, at a fixed instant the normalized complex frequency correlation is `(1+exp(−i2πΔfτ))/2` up to conjugation convention. The received-power correlation is `cos²(πΔfτ)`. At `τ=1 ms`, `Δf=12.5 Hz` gives about 0.9985, 250 Hz gives 0.5, and 500 Hz gives zero in the toy model. At 1 kHz it comes back to one because the two paths' phase separation wraps. Adjacent alphabet tones may have distinct correlations at the detector and still share essentially the same fading. Wide hopping can visit other fades given the underlying delay-power distribution, and all bins can remain in an absorption outage. Prefer measuring `|R_f|²` across the actual usable band and lag before selecting the diversity profile. [Existing two-path reference](../../../docs/channel-models.md#midlatitude-moderate-hf).

## Compare complete factorizations

| Route | Matrix work | When it could improve service | Main discriminator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct dense bank | Packed time windows × quadrature templates; fuse norm, score and per-window reductions if supported | Large irregular grids, many same-template views, low packing cost, or a template basis with no cheaper factorization | Count useful unique projections and all eligible hypothesis scores, plus bytes and ready-to-commit latency. Compare implicit window gathering with materialized rows. |
| Channelize once, evaluate locally | Reusable transform or polyphase analysis, then small packed matrices for candidate residual frequencies/time and optional subspace combinations | Search paths share the same spectral bins or adjacent offset hypotheses; phase and filter memory retained | Check tone/window equivalence at a band edge, between bins, under resampling, and through hop/delay boundaries. A four-bit transform requires its own arithmetic proof. |
| Direct CPU slab | Same templates using scalar nibble operations, then a qualified native vectorized or bitplane implementation with the same numerical contract | Modest grids, little batching latency, integrated/shared memory limits, or unavailable Tensor Core route | Compare identical observation receipts, returned labels and workload coverage; pin arithmetic identity and include scheduling/packing/commit time. |
| Sliding or factored special case | Update a fixed tone filter per next window or use shared frequency grids and short local matrices | Dense overlapping time starts dominate and recurrence or FFT reduces work at the same score quality | Check state drift, edge updates, cancellation and quantized accumulation, then benchmark with complete scoring. |

The uniform rectangular grid is special. A 512-sample row at 3,200 samples/s has a native 6.25 Hz DFT grid. On a 1.5625 Hz frequency grid a length-2,048 zero-padded DFT assesses that row at the same frequencies, up to FFT arithmetic and the choice to quantize the templates. A radix-2 transform uses `2,048×11/2 = 11,264` complex butterflies versus roughly 721,000 complex products for 1,409 direct tones per row, before job sharing, taper, four-bit roundoff and epilogue costs. Thus even with fast matrix instructions, outputs on an actual regular grid deserve a factored baseline. Matrix processing can still compute selected bins or non-grid small transforms. FP16 Tensor Core FFT research establishes a useful layout precedent, with different arithmetic. Published PFB implementations also show the real cost of the analysis filter and outputs. [Li, Cheng and Lin, tcFFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11471), [Price, spectrometers and PFBs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03579), [GPU PFB spectrometer](https://doi.org/10.1017/pasa.2014.43).

An illustrative dense tile with `B=256`, `N=512`, `F=384` uses 402,653,184 integer operations when a multiply and addition count separately. The packed input and template total 524,288 bytes; storing two signed 32-bit projections per window/frequency adds 786,432 bytes. These are ideal logical bytes per tile with neither gather traffic nor reuse across tiles. Fusing rejection/score on device can eliminate most projection spills; retaining all of the full candidate products across alignments can make output bandwidth the limiting resource. A full 1.5625 Hz grid across 2,200 Hz has up to 1,409 frequencies before edge guards. At 125 eligible starts/s and 512 complex samples/window, direct evaluation of that grid costs about 0.72 billion integer ops/s, before independent drift, paths, shape banks or software overhead. A tightly configured 6.25 starts/s would cost about 0.036 billion ops/s. These ordinary one-stream examples give little work relative to a multi-TOPS accelerator. Qualification should fill batches with required broad-search, genuinely independent hypotheses, or simulations, and measure the benefit of redundant operations only where they advance complete useful service. [Device-specific throughput and shape caveats](int4-and-accelerators.md).

## Native pipeline and bounded overlap

Run one C++ library and standalone appraisal entry point, with no modem changes to Fast, Robust or Legacy. A future integration can call the verified shared keyfile and device interfaces in-process. Keep the experiment source and its test documentation under `_appraisal/algorithms_matrixModem/`. The app options should attach to measured quantities: fixed transmit profile, timing knowledge, search range and gap budget, DATA/Helper power, nameplate RF carrier and shift, filter and audio device, supported compute route, and whether each on-air configuration was qualified. Simulation-only truth and oscillator/fading/noise controls belong on a separate input. [Native processing contract](../../../docs/development.md#native-processing-and-integration).

1. One continuous sample ingress owns acquisition and sample-validity/timestamps. Front-end/filter state stays ordered per input. Split early at immutable quantized windows or tiles with halos. DDC and analysis kernels can use disjoint output ranges when exact front-end state/overlap is provided. Freeze the search geometry for each admitted job and retain original time and ordinal coordinates.
2. Use one bounded CPU executor with default `W=2H` for effective logical hardware count `H`, plus explicit OS affinity/quota and operator limits. Queued prep and row packing can advance while earlier device kernels run. Earlier ready slots whose results are required for commit get priority. Work counters distinguish preparation, device submit, waiting and useful CPU/GPU execution. Per-tile bound and buffers should cover peak live workspace, with exact backpressure, and no unbounded pageable or pinned slab list. [Project CPU allocation and owner lifetimes](../../../docs/development.md#receiver-cpu-allocation).
3. Compare double/triple reusable pinned staging with device-resident templates, overlapping copy/compute where actual hardware permits. A device ring and tiled gathers can reduce overlapping window copies; consumer iGPUs need separate shared-bandwidth accounting. Bucket shapes, check the tail of each batch, set deadline and maximum batch wait, and reduce/gather likelihoods near the projection. Only an actual device run with instruction and operation receipts qualifies the route. Maintain an exact compatible CPU path with explicit fallback, and verify that a required GPU command fails or reports unavailable if the device route cannot execute.
4. Cleanly separate ready parallel scoring from causal tracking, null validation, decoder commit and completion. Update tracking from committed observations only, or attach a snapshot/version with a defined recheck if a stale state changes public work. Release completed scratch before ordered commit if result storage is bounded independently. Track input backlog and ready-to-commit latency through failed Helper, broad noise-only intervals and reacquisition, as well as through successful DATA. The accepted rate includes all that work.

Develop with one calibrated source of truth for rendered samples, observation spans, public candidates and decisions. Use a full scalar small-radix reference for bounded equivalence, an optional analysis-only likelihood oracle for error attribution, and a few actual-device cost cases. A better mathematical statistic or implementation can supersede this route if it beats the matched complete-work comparison.
