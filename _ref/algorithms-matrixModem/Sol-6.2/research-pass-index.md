24 November 2026. This appraisal evaluates FT8-inspired tone signaling, repeated and hopped patterns, and a high-throughput native receiver for weak and disturbed HF links. It combines primary literature, measured propagation studies, prior repository experiments and new bounded calculations. Its design suggestions are candidates for matched testing. Better waveforms, detectors and factorizations remain possible.

## Findings that change the design

- **Start with a shaped FSK reference and a complete tone-bank search.** FT8 demonstrates how shaping, synchronization, constrained messages and soft coding work together. Its 6.25 Hz increment and 160 ms duration give a useful control. They do not guarantee orthogonality after filtering, clock error, delayed modes or Doppler. At −10 dB-Hz the ideal energy in 160 ms is only −17.96 dB relative to the noise density. One-symbol decisions at that level are a poor proxy for the long-code calculation. Keep whole apertures, shorter analysis segments and longer transmitted symbols as distinct branches. [Waveform evidence](waveform-and-repetition-review.md), [link budget](link-budget-and-operation-review.md).
- **The fading numbers have a specific, demanding set of assumptions.** The 5.39, 14.09, 23.92 and 28.68 dB values reproduce for the four Gaussian widths in the prompt. A hard time peak equal to the average power, no CSI, flat Rayleigh fading and asymptotically long coding support a conditional converse. General time-frequency-selective WSSUS, finite bandwidth, specular/mixed paths, different peak budgets and finite deadlines require their own bounds or experiments. The earlier 97–99.8% is a rate ratio for stored idealized MFSK sweeps with idealized interleaving and synchronization. [Theory and calculation audit](information-limits-review.md).
- **Protect against several failure mechanisms.** Propagation shifts, delay spread, within-path Doppler, slowly rotating path cancellation, impulses, intermodulation and a closed route change different parts of the problem. Standards and measured sources establish physically grounded strata. They do not order all channels by one severity scalar. A receiver that stays in range should retain plausible public hypotheses, report unresolved intervals, and reacquire without allowing a transport or resource event to stand in for a physical quiet slot. An outage can be observationally identical to the end of transmission. [HF envelope](channel-envelope-review.md), [physical completion and measurement](link-budget-and-operation-review.md).
- **Test repetition as optional statistical evidence.** Two already associated observations can benefit from the right covariance model when they share usable fading/phase history. Reusing a raw noisy waveform adds noise × noise, and a reusable reference can fail after a hop, fade or oscillator slip. In the single Gaussian tap calculation at 6.25 Hz full width, the 160 ms whole-tone projection retains roughly 60% of mean energy. Adjacent projected signal correlation is about 0.17, and at a two-slot lag it is about 0.00010. A known or otherwise independently acquired reference remains a valuable control. Compare repeated and parity-linked alternatives at equal power, elapsed time and total energy as explicitly separate regimes. [Window covariance derivation](receiver-and-evaluation-review.md#channel-memory-at-the-receivers-actual-aperture), [native two-look screen](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/repetition-screen/README.md).
- **Report detectability per observer.** In the idealized 352-tone, one-look example at false alarm `10⁻³` and detection probability `0.9`, the broadband radiometer needs 19.55 dB `E/N₀`, a known path 10.76 dB, and a 352-schedule search 12.90 dB. All are for a single observation model. Across multiple looks, more required total energy can coexist with less required mean power because the observation lasts longer. Real observers may exploit stationary, repetitive or cyclostationary structure with a different antenna and propagation path. Encryption changes the knowledge of labels; observer tests must measure signal presence independently of whether the labels can be read. [Observer literature](observer-models-review.md), [frequency span plot](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/observer-screen/results/detectability.svg), [listening-time plot](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/observer-screen/results/detectability-cn0.svg).
- **Exploit exact structure before doing more matrix work.** Regular-grid FFT/gather or channelization can share work across labels, schedules and uncertainty hypotheses. On a full RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition, FP32 cuFFT plus gather took 4.1–5.1× less time than the reused NVFP4 path across six regular-grid projection shapes. That stage comparison included NVFP4 packing and zero-padded contraction. The provider denied performance-counter access, so dynamic Tensor utilization remains unknown. Dense products deserve a separate comparison for truly irregular templates, covariance modes and codewords. Factorization, quantization and candidate pruning change different quantities. Cross-device peak numbers or saturating a large synthetic GEMM can characterize hardware while live readiness, complete search, timely ordered decisions and memory admission still need their own measurements. [Native candidates and qualification](receiver-and-evaluation-review.md), [standalone projection probe](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/projection-probe/README.md).

## Implication for first implementation

Use a bounded configured calendar and explicit tone passband in a standalone native appraisal. Preserve the timing/frequency uncertainty coverage across arithmetic and detector variants. Start with directly checkable short quiet/active/missing slot cases, then test physical delays and independent channel evolution on the same TX energy and complete-codeword deadlines. Keep fixed-bank energy as a calibrated fallback while comparing segmented and covariance scoring. Carry the strict INT4 interpretation from the original brief as a distinct research constraint. The wide-accumulator GPU comparator cannot silently settle that operating decision. [Evaluation sequence](receiver-and-evaluation-review.md#bounded-validation-sequence), [original Matrix brief](../../../_appraisal/matrixModem.md).

The present work supports narrowing the next experiments. A complete Matrix Modem, field coverage, successful acquisition of unknown epochs, strict-INT4 deployment, and operational LPI remain unqualified. Accept any later candidate on calibrated communication and false-alarm outcomes, physical completion and full receiver service through moderate and difficult cases. Peak fit or average cost alone is insufficient where an uncontrolled case can cause abrupt sensitivity loss or sustained backlog.
