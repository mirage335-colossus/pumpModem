# One-carrier waveforms and Tensor Core receivers

24 November 2026 · Matrix Modem literature check

I found academic receivers that use dense neural operations for one-tone-at-a-time MFSK and academic implementations that use CUDA for FSK acquisition and demodulation. The closest verified MFSK experiments used GPUs from before NVIDIA introduced Tensor Cores. I found nearby single-trajectory waveform and receiver co-design in chirp spread spectrum. Its low-precision Tensor Core performance remains unverified in the sources examined. I found no academic result that demonstrated effective Tensor Core MFSK reception, or explicitly selected a one-tone-at-a-time MFSK transmission format for those units.

Here one carrier at a time means one oscillator frequency trajectory per transmitter, including finite tone steps, Gaussian-shaped continuous-phase FSK, and the separate case of a swept chirp. A physical channel can introduce multiple delayed copies and multiple transmitters can overlap. That does not convert each transmitter's waveform into an intentional simultaneous multicarrier signal. Hardware evidence requires documented Tensor operations in the receive path, with matched receive measurements for a performance claim.

| Kozlenko and Vialkova, IEEE TCSET 2020 | JT65A-style orthogonal 64-FSK. A dense `4096 → 256 → 128 → 64` network detects one symbol from synthesized, random-phase AWGN windows. | Tesla K80 GPU inference and CPU inference were timed. This corroborates that an actual one-tone receiver can use GPU neural calculations. It is a second receiver topology from the same research group, with the same hardware-generation limit. [Paper, §§III–VI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.19097) |

The dense JT65A paper's sensitivity comparison in Fig. 10 uses the binary orthogonal noncoherent-FSK BER formula in Eq. (8). Its timing result is useful for identifying actual GPU demodulation. Its quoted dB gap needs a corresponding optimal 64-tone receiver comparison. [Paper, §VII](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.16371), [authors' notebook](https://github.com/kozlenko/jt65)

FT8 is a strong format precedent for the user’s waveform constraint. It selects among eight tones on one continuous-phase frequency trajectory, using a Gray label map and synchronization sequences. A separately developed open-source receiver, GranolaSDR, reports GPU FT8/JS8 acquisition on an RTX 5060. Its inspected Costas search uses ordinary CUDA threads, shared loads and per-hypothesis tone scores. The project documents a CUDA FFT/channelizer and a CPU decoding stage. The actual use of Tensor Cores by other library-internal operations remains unmeasured. [FT4 and FT8 specification](https://wsjt.sourceforge.io/FT4_FT8_QEX.pdf), [GranolaSDR architecture](https://github.com/granolamatt/GranolaSDR), [Costas kernel](https://github.com/granolamatt/GranolaSDR/blob/main/gm/cuda/FT8ScanCuda.cu)

Some space-telemetry papers cover a broader signal than strictly one data-tone trajectory. JPL's Mars Exploration Rover design used a residual carrier together with a selected subcarrier tone. Its receiver acquired and tracked the carrier and then searched the data tones using FFT-based processing. The separation is relevant for acquisition under rapid dynamics. The residual carrier uses transmit power and changes the waveform budget. [JPL test and design report](https://ipnpr.jpl.nasa.gov/progress_report/42-153/153A.pdf)

## Single-trajectory results near the requested combination

- **Morph, 2025 preprint.** Different numbers of sequential up-chirps at four sweep rates encode two bits over one SF12-duration symbol, using a commercial LoRa transmitter. Standard SF12 maps 12 raw bits into that interval. The authors made the receive inference problem smaller with a sixfold reduction in raw bits per interval. They report about 8 ms versus 11 ms for decoding a 16-symbol packet with Morph versus their adapted neural reference on a GTX 1050 Ti. This is a close waveform-to-computation precedent in chirp spread spectrum, measured on a pre-Tensor Pascal GPU. [Morph, §§III–V](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22851), [author manuscript, Fig. 10](https://cse.msu.edu/~caozc/papers/arxiv25-ren.pdf)
- **PreCo, IEEE WoWMoM 2025.** The receiver enumerates constrained short LoRaWAN packets and coherently tests sequences of chirps. At 5% fitted packet error, it reports about 3.1 dB average SNR benefit over its standard decoder. On an A100, one SF7 example took 0.16 s for synchronization plus correlation and 3.20 s including candidate preparation, against 0.024 s for the conventional receiver on a 0.19 s IQ window. Section IV uses elementwise broadcast products and per-column FFTs. A matrix-shaped array and an A100 are evidence of GPU parallelism here. The paper gives no Tensor Core mode or activity result. This is the closest checked analogue of testing many plausible messages as a bank. [PreCo, §§IV–VI](https://www.areinhardt.de/publications/2025/Szafranski_WoWMoM_2025.pdf)
- **LoRaSeek, ACM MobiCom 2025.** A neural denoising receiver for standard LoRa reports GPU inference on an RTX 3090 for captured chirps. Full model inference takes 3.8–14.7 ms for 16 chirps across the reported spreading factors. This narrows the hardware gap relative to Morph and the K80 studies. The paper reports per-model inference time, without a Tensor Core kernel or utilization attribution. [LoRaSeek, §5.3 and Table I](https://www.cse.msu.edu/~caozc/papers/mobicom25-khang.pdf)
- **Constant-envelope CPM sequence selection, IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025.** Yuan and colleagues encode bits by selecting cyclically shifted, quasi-orthogonal continuous-phase spread sequences. This provides a concrete alternative to selecting one fixed tone per symbol, while retaining a single phase trajectory in the point-to-point variant. Simulations study BER and complexity. Cyclic structure already permits correlation reuse, and the results do not establish a Tensor-specific implementation. [Yuan et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10290v1)
- **Hybrid CSS-DSSS CPFSK, IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025.** Wen and colleagues use opposite-slope preamble chirps to simplify synchronization and CPFSK spread payloads to retain envelope and phase continuity. They implemented a noncoherent despreading/demodulation receiver with GNU Radio and USRP and reported field trials. Their format addresses sensitivity and receiver simplicity. The published implementation does not identify Tensor acceleration. [Wen et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.23029v1), [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2025.3595159)
- **LR-FHSS, 2025.** Jung and colleagues implement a GMSK transmitter that switches hop channels over time, plus a receiver that searches a broad bank and tracks timing and Doppler. The described system has up to 3,120 hop channels and uses soft-output Viterbi processing. The proof of concept uses an ASIC and FPGA. The example shows how a one-hop-at-a-time transmission creates substantial useful receiver work through uncertainty and multiple terminals. [Jung et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14154), [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/OJVT.2025.3585160)

The broader 2022 V100 Tensor Core PN-pilot study establishes actual matrix correlation in a transmitter-and-receiver design, using simultaneous multiplexed pilots and multiple antenna signals for channel estimation. Generic Tensor Core FFT libraries benchmark Fourier transforms. A Tensor Core Viterbi implementation also uses V100 matrix instructions for batches of branch metrics, with BPSK-derived soft inputs in its performance/error tests. These are verified accelerator precedents for separable receiver stages. Applying them to an MFSK receiver still requires an MFSK-specific complete-work comparison. [Gokalgandhi and Seskar, §§II–IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.05506v1), [tcFFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11471), [Mohammadidoost and Hashemi](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13579)

A November 2026 GNSS preprint is an adjacent single-antenna acquisition example. Suzuki uses small matrix products for four-step DFTs and measures Doppler/code-phase acquisition on AX8850 and RK3588 NPUs. For 21 transforms of size 16,000, the two matrix-DFT stages on AX8850 take 0.88 ms versus 3.08 ms for the compared radix FFT model, despite more arithmetic. This is a real-radio search analogue using a different accelerator and spread-spectrum waveform. [Suzuki, §§5–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18186v1)

The 2025 SIMS preprint is a block-waveform and parallel-receiver co-design that explicitly discusses GPUs. It represents ordinary FSK as choosing one inverse-DFT basis row. The proposed block scheme is more general, and its correlation algorithm exploits FFT structure. The evidence consists of simulation, complexity estimates and implementation rationale. It does not demonstrate Tensor execution or show that its full block scheme meets a strict one-tone constraint. [SIMS, §§II, IV and V](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.20364v1)

Earlier work by Hosseini and Perrins also illustrates transmitter design that removes receive work. A known CPM preamble lets the receiver estimate joint timing, carrier phase and frequency from a one-dimensional grid search and closed-form estimates. The study covers burst-mode CPM with simulated AWGN. This is a useful algorithmic comparison when deciding how much search should be left for matrix acceleration. [Hosseini and Perrins](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.0757)

## Implication for Matrix Modem

For regular orthogonal MFSK, the useful per-symbol evidence comes from a small set of Fourier projections. A shared FFT, short filter bank or sliding projection can generate the evidence without calculating a separate dot product for each candidate in a regular grid. A bank of many genuinely different time-frequency paths, pulse shapes or delay–Doppler channel templates can expose more reusable matrix work. Keeping one transmitted tone or phase trajectory does not preclude these receiver dimensions. Their usefulness depends on how much uncertainty they resolve within the same latency and energy budget.

If a receiver has independent per-symbol likelihoods `L[k,m]`, the score of known coded tone sequence `c` is `sum_k L[k,c[k]]`. This is an indexed sum, or a matrix product with a codebook having one nonzero weight per symbol. Binary FSK has a special dense mapping because the two alternatives can be represented as a row term plus one signed contrast per symbol. The in-repository GPU result measured that exact binary case. Larger tone alphabets need a competitive sparse gather, structured transform or actual soft FEC baseline. A bigger bank can create more arithmetic without improving communication. [Binary-FSK GPU experiment](tensor-reception-results.md), [MFSK factoring and channel caveats](received-history-design.md), [same-work cache measurements](tensor-receiver-bottlenecks.md)

An exact transform can also apply when the transmit code has suitable structure. For (M=2^b) and an affine binary tone-label map `c[k] = G[k] w ⊕ d[k]`, take a small Walsh transform of each (M)-point metric table. The coefficient indexed by (v) contributes to the payload Walsh coordinate `G[k]ᵀv`, with a sign set by `v·d[k]`. Sum these coefficients over the frame and use one Walsh–Hadamard transform over payload indices to obtain every word score. This follows by expanding the symbol metric in binary characters. It is an arithmetic alternative conditional on separable likelihoods and the chosen code structure. Code distance, channel memory, detection accuracy and device latency need their own evidence. [Binary linear soft-decoding precedent](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1986.1057189)

## Search scope and access

The targeted search used MFSK, orthogonal FSK, CPFSK, GFSK, GMSK, CPM, JT65, FT8 and one-chirp CSS/LoRa with combinations of Tensor Core, WMMA/MMA, cuBLAS, CUTLASS, CUDA, TensorRT, mixed precision, GPU inference, demodulation and receiver. I checked available author manuscripts, publisher records, academic search, original GPU specifications and public receiver source where available. Hardware details for the 2025 Sevillano et al. feed-forward MFSK receiver remain open because the verifiable conference record and related CTTC project catalog do not describe the execution hardware and the full paper could not be retrieved. GPU signal classification, offline training and FEC measurements were evaluated separately from payload demodulation.
