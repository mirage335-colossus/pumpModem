# Alternatives to MFSK on weak, disturbed links

25 November 2026 · Literature review

**Some alternatives materially improve reception under specific impairments.** The strongest field comparison found was coherent frequency-repetition spread spectrum against noncoherent MFSK in severe acoustic multipath. A one-trajectory alternative, chirp spread spectrum, beat MFSK in controlled simulations with persistent frequency-selective fading. Phase-based signaling has a substantial sensitivity advantage over binary orthogonal FSK when phase can be tracked, and a 2026 single-oscillator design beat rate-matched coded binary GFSK in an IoT hardware study. Across the surveyed studies, a substantial end-to-end computation advantage over well-implemented coded MFSK remains unverified for weak, disturbed HF with uncertain acquisition. None of the studies establishes that an alternative wins on both axes across that envelope.

## Evidence specific to HF

Watterson's NTIA study identifies partially coherent PSK as an effective choice among the HF systems it evaluated. Its Fig. 19 gives a 3 dB coherent-PSK advantage over coherent binary FSK in the low-error limit of a slow, flat Rayleigh path. The plotted FSK curve is for binary signaling. The design discussion also favors adaptive receive filtering, receiver diversity and soft decoding, and distinguishes partial carrier coherence from time-differential PSK. This is favorable evidence for a phase-based branch when phase estimates are available, with a substantially narrower comparison than a current weak-signal MFSK frame test. [Watterson, NTIA TR-79-29, §§ 6.1 and 7.1, printed pp. 80–81 and 115–118](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-C60-PURL-gpo29430/pdf/GOVPUB-C60-PURL-gpo29430.pdf)

Jodalen et al. measured high-latitude channel conditions and used modem-simulator characterizations to estimate availability. Their 75 bit/s STANAG 4415 and 4285 modes gave 60–75% higher availability than the 2400 bit/s STANAG 4285 mode. The low-rate modes largely retained that availability through a geomagnetic disturbance. STANAG 4415 is a spread single-carrier mode with Walsh signaling, convolutional coding and interleaving. The 32-fold rate difference and absence of MFSK in the comparison make this evidence for low-rate HF robustness. [Jodalen et al., Radio Science, 2001](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2000RS002547), [Bergadà et al., § 2](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/2013rs005203)

The Antarctica–Spain experiments reported that DSSS achieved a given observed raw BER with 12–14 dB less SNR than the OFDM configurations, while OFDM supported about 10–15 times the bit rate at equal bandwidth. These are different operating points on the measured HF path, with different waveforms, receiver details and spectral efficiencies. Increasing DSSS integration time also eventually hurt BER as the symbols approached channel coherence time. This evidence supports a spread single-carrier comparison in HF and illustrates how a rate difference can account for much of an apparent sensitivity gain. [Bergadà et al., Radio Science, 2014, Tables 1–3 and conclusions](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/2013rs005203)

I found no recent peer-reviewed HF head-to-head that holds usable payload, airtime, power, bandwidth and acquisition uncertainty close enough to quantify the gain over a strong coded noncoherent MFSK baseline across moderate and severe channels. The comparison above includes original technical reports, link experiments, direct simulation comparisons, and recent results from other frequency bands and propagation regimes. A result with long acoustic delays, a static RF notch or large common oscillator drift has a different transfer condition in each case.

## Why binary comparisons and spreading gains differ

An uncoded AWGN calculation at 1% BER shows the effect of changing the FSK order. Using ideal symbol timing, known frequency, coherent BPSK and noncoherent maximum-energy orthogonal FSK gives the following `Eb/N₀` thresholds. The values come from solving the BPSK error formula `Q(sqrt(2 Eb/N₀))` and the exact orthogonal FSK sum in Eq. (2.3) of Hernandez. [Naval Postgraduate School thesis, § II](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8d/Digital_communications_over_non-fading_and_fading_channels_%28IA_digitalcommunica109453899%29.pdf)

| Detector | Required `Eb/N₀` |
| --- | ---: |
| Coherent BPSK | 4.32 dB |
| Noncoherent 2-FSK | 8.93 dB |
| Noncoherent 4-FSK | 6.50 dB |
| Noncoherent 8-FSK | 5.25 dB |
| Noncoherent 16-FSK | 4.45 dB |
| Noncoherent 64-FSK | 3.48 dB |

This illustrates a 4.61 dB advantage over binary FSK and a 0.85 dB disadvantage against 64-FSK at this operating point. Higher-order FSK needs a larger time–bandwidth product to fit orthogonal tones. The rows omit coding, phase recovery, pilots and packet acquisition. Under a single slow flat Rayleigh fade, differential BPSK and noncoherent orthogonal BFSK both have BER inversely proportional to average `Eb/N₀` at high SNR. With mean `Eb/N₀` equal to `γ`, the binary formulas are `1/[2(1+γ)]` for DBPSK with a path stable over the pair and `1/(2+γ)` for noncoherent orthogonal BFSK. The difference approaches 3 dB on this per-bit energy axis. Rapid phase changes between the differential reference and data can erode this gain. Coding across independent fades can improve the error-rate slope for either family. [Hernandez, §§ II–III](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8d/Digital_communications_over_non-fading_and_fading_channels_%28IA_digitalcommunica109453899%29.pdf), [Tse and Viswanath, § 3.1](https://web.stanford.edu/~dntse/papers/main.pdf)

CSS can be viewed as a set of orthogonal tones multiplied by a common unit-magnitude chirp in the ideal discrete-time model. Dechirping is a unitary operation, so a correctly aligned AWGN comparison with equal signal energy has equal noise and decision statistics. A real delayed path does not generally commute with that multiplication. A chirp distributes a symbol's energy across frequencies and can survive a static null that erases one fixed tone. Coded hopping MFSK can supply frequency diversity across symbols. Adjacent 6.25 Hz tones are not independent diversity branches by default. For example, two paths separated by 5 ms produce a frequency-response ripple period of 200 Hz. That period is set by the different path arrival times, even if the symbol dwell is 160 ms. [Vangelista, IEEE Signal Processing Letters, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1109/LSP.2017.2762960), [Marquet et al., § 5.3](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0140366419314665)

Multipath can also drive a conventional chirp detector to an error floor. In a 2026 LoRa study, a detector that combines bins using channel statistics held up in a 5 Hz maximum-Doppler simulation where preamble-based channel estimates became stale. It uses a dechirp and transform plus per-symbol combining over modeled taps, and its estimated channel statistics cost sensitivity in the weaker direct-path cases. This is a receiver comparison within CSS at 500 kHz nominal bandwidth. It shows why the choice of channel model and combining rule affects the viability of the chirp alternative. [Nguyen, Bedeer and Barton, IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, 2026, §§ III–IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13778v2)

Recent work also strengthened the baseline. Gómez-Vilardebó et al. derived soft MFSK metrics for unknown Doppler frequency offset and evaluated them with LDPC on a nonfading space-link model. In a separate 2026 solar-scintillation simulation, 16-FSK reduced the reported `Eb/N₀` threshold relative to 2-FSK by 5.3 dB uncoded and 1.9 dB with the study's LDPC code carrying 256 information bits at a frame-error target of 10⁻⁵. That code was originally designed for coherent PSK in AWGN. The project's [information-limit review](information-limits-review.md) gives the stationary Rayleigh and peak-power conditions for which frequency-peaky signaling is already strong. [Gómez-Vilardebó et al., IEEE JSAC, 2024](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10436069), [Zeqaj et al., Radio Science, 2026, § 5.3 and Table 5](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2025RS008358), [Guillén i Fàbregas and Grant, capacity-approaching MFSK codes](https://www.upf.edu/documents/221459052/223130115/ncfsk_twc.pdf)

Carrier tracking can reverse the theoretical PSK advantage. In Modestino's NASA planetary-entry channel study, coded noncoherent MFSK outperformed coded coherent BPSK when phase tracking errors were included in the model. The study identifies uncertainty in the underlying phase-perturbation process and the benefits of interleaving for correlated fading. Recent polar-coded acoustic MFSK work likewise reports better robustness with wider tone separation, or a lower code rate and longer frame, on measured-channel replay. Those are distinct effects from improving residual carrier offset. [NASA workshop paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19750012325), [Lidström, IEEE Journal of Oceanic Engineering](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10049248/)

## Receiver computation and Matrix Modem

- **Tracked phase can make payload decisions cheaper.** Coherent BPSK or QPSK can use one complex matched-pulse observation per symbol with an available timing and carrier estimate. The simple phase decision then uses one or two components. Orthogonal MFSK requires a collection of tone statistics. Direct projections cost order `MN` for `M` tones and `N` samples per window. A regular grid can share an FFT or a channelizer. Total work can reverse when the phase mode must search, reacquire, equalize changing paths or pay for pilots. These are operation counts and architectural inferences. [Tse and Viswanath, § 3.1](https://web.stanford.edu/~dntse/papers/main.pdf), [Matrix receiver factorizations](receiver-architecture.md#compare-complete-factorizations)
- **Differential BPSK uses the previous received symbol as its reference.** Given two consecutive matched-filter outputs, the receiver takes the real part of their complex conjugate product and checks its sign after compensating frequency rotation. This costs a constant number of operations per symbol after filtering. In the same flat Rayleigh model the leading BER terms at large `γ` are about `0.500/γ` for DBPSK and `0.494/γ` for noncoherent 8-FSK. The latter follows from the exact MFSK sum with `Es/N₀ = 3γ`. Sklar's published comparison likewise places the curves within 0.1 dB. This is a conditional example of similar sensitivity with fewer payload projections, using a stable path across adjacent DBPSK symbols, correct symbol boundaries, compensated frequency offset, and no coding. Both observations contain receiver noise. Doppler spreading and residual oscillator rotation can turn that reference into poor evidence. [Sklar, IEEE Communications Magazine](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCOM.1997.621038), [Tse and Viswanath, § 3.1.4](https://web.stanford.edu/~dntse/papers/main.pdf)
- **A chirp can improve acquisition geometry while adding a demodulation operation.** A standard CSS receiver dechirps then transforms. A time error can look like a frequency error for a single sweep direction. Opposite sweep directions can separate the two effects when the relevant paths retain a stable peak across the preamble. Heitmann et al. measured synchronization within 10% of a symbol in 83.8% of chirp trials versus 67.9% for their noncoherent FSK detector in shallow-water multipath. Their operation-count analysis charges more for chirp correlation and their experiment measures sync accuracy. [Busacca et al., SDR-LoRa synchronization design, § 4.2](https://www.iris.unict.it/bitstream/20.500.11769/590289/1/SDR%20LoRa%201-s2.0-S1389128624000264-main.pdf), [Heitmann, Steinmetz and Renner, §§ III–IV](https://tore.tuhh.de/dspace-cris-server/api/core/bitstreams/479884e3-db12-4fa7-b3bf-03cf71c2d7f2/content)
- **Longer memory has a cost and can still be factored.** CPM can exploit phase continuity using finite-state sequence detection, and Laurent pulse decompositions can reduce the matched-filter work. For frequency diversity, FRSS can recover delayed-path information with adaptive equalization. Its documented reliability advantage does not give a CPU or accelerator throughput result. Likewise, parallel GPU blocks and high Tensor Core activity cannot establish a lower end-to-end receiver cost for a different waveform. [Laurent, IEEE Transactions on Communications, 1986](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCOM.1986.1096504), [Arctic field study, § 3.3](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2026.1878182/full), [Matrix Tensor Core receiver evidence](tensor-receiver-bottlenecks.md)
- **Staged hypotheses can save work without changing waveform family.** Lidström tested a polar-coded permutation FSK design on seven measured-channel replays and a high-mobility shallow-water field link. The receiver tests arrival time, Doppler and likelihood parameters in increasing order of cost, and only runs more expensive stages on surviving hypotheses. Table I gives operation counts. The design uses parallel carriers and an initial power statistic, whose pruning and false-alarm behavior need separate qualification at Matrix Modem's below-noise operating point. [Lidström, Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 2024, §§ III.E–IV](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0025130)

For a single-trajectory Matrix Modem comparison, the strongest justified alternatives are coded low-order PSK with charged phase recovery, and a chirped or phase-spread candidate with a factored detector. PSK addresses trackable channels, including common oscillator drift that can be estimated. CSS or phase spreading addresses persistent frequency-selective fading. For pronounced Doppler spread, the appropriate baseline is soft, interleaved, frequency-diverse MFSK with short coherent looks and noncoherent or partial-coherence combining. Received-history correlation can propose smaller relative drift searches while successive copies share useful channel structure. Each extra copy uses some of the energy or airtime budget, and such a proposal still requires a bounded path through missed acquisition and rapid decorrelation. [Matrix drift results and conditions](received-history-drift.md), [HF channel profiles and measured limits](channel-envelope-review.md)

The relevant comparison counts the same useful payload, peak transmit power, transmitted energy, occupied bandwidth and deadline. Sensitivity means correct frame delivery including acquisition at a stated false-alarm rate. Receiver cost means sustained input service including empty intervals, false candidates, tracking failures, and complete decoding on one stream. Those conditions are needed to tell a cheaper symbol detector, a diversity benefit, and a cheaper complete receiver apart.
