# Native receiver candidates and how to discriminate them


Conduct the first comparisons on equal public work. Keep the same incoming samples, aperture, legal tone/epoch/offset alternatives, uncertainty, and null. Change the factorization or arithmetic one at a time. A higher throughput for incomplete search can hide an avoidable sensitivity cliff. [Previous Matrix design](../../algorithms-matrixModem/Sol-6.2/receiver-architecture.md), [complete-service and precision contract](../../../docs/gpu-precision-controls.md#automatic-mode-prioritizes-computational-ceiling).

## Channel memory at the receiver's actual aperture

For a unit-power flat tap with symmetric Gaussian Doppler PSD of full width `w = 2σ`, its point-sample correlation is `R(t) = exp(−π²w²t²/2)`. A rectangular, correctly de-rotated tone projection averages the tap over the aperture. Its covariance for window separation `Δ` is

`C_T(Δ) = ∫[-1,1] (1−|u|) R(Δ+Tu) du`.

`C_T(0)` is the fraction of mean wanted energy delivered to the whole-aperture constant-tone projection. `C_T(Δ)/C_T(0)` is the correlation of the two *signal components* after projection. In additive noise with independent per-window noise of unit variance and per-window projected signal power `ρ`, the total observed correlation for disjoint windows is `ρ C_T(Δ)/[(1+ρ) C_T(0)]`. At very weak received power, that last factor can be small even with strong underlying channel correlation. These identities follow by integrating the covariance over two windows. They omit multipath, filter correlations, path phase, CFO, clock error and unequal apertures. [F.1487 width convention](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-F.1487/en), [earlier projection energy calculation](../../algorithms-matrixModem/Sol-6.2/receiver-architecture.md#timefrequency-checks-before-an-implementation-choice).

Midpoint integration over `u∈[−1,1]` with 20,000 steps gives these checks at `T=160 ms`. `Δ=T` uses neighboring disjoint windows, so their nearest edges can still share coherent channel even when the midpoint samples correlate weakly.

| `w` | Whole-tone mean energy change | Point `R(T)` | Projected signal correlation `C_T(T)/C_T(0)` | Projected ratio at `Δ=2T` |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.5 Hz | −0.023 dB | 0.969 | 0.969 | 0.882 |
| 1 Hz | −0.090 dB | 0.881 | 0.886 | 0.616 |
| 2 Hz | −0.345 dB | 0.603 | 0.651 | 0.178 |
| 6.25 Hz | −2.252 dB | 0.00719 | 0.170 | 0.000098 |
| 10 Hz | −3.772 dB | 0.00000326 | 0.0943 | 1.10×10⁻⁸ |
| 30 Hz | −8.029 dB | <10⁻⁸ | 0.0279 | <10⁻⁸ |

At 6.25 Hz spread the same 160 ms observation still captures about 59.5% of the mean energy through its constant-frequency template. A two-symbol lag carries almost no mean correlation under this model. These are different mechanisms. Use the projected covariance in a test of history-assisted detection and measure the energy term separately. A two-look screen in this appraisal shows when covariance helps and when the cross-product alone loses sensitivity. It assumes already associated observations. Hops between frequency-selective fades can change the picture in either direction. [Two-look results](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/repetition-screen/README.md), [waveform and repetition](waveform-and-repetition-review.md).

### Mean leakage into other labels

The same integral also checks tone separability for a correctly timed single rectangular tone. The fraction of mean wanted energy in a projection at offset `δf` is `2∫[0,1](1−u)R(Tu)cos(2πδfTu)du`. Divide by `C_T(0)` to compare with the centered projection on matched input. With the same 20,000-point midpoint quadrature this gives:

| Doppler width | Ratio at 6.25 Hz offset | At 12.5 Hz | At 18.75 Hz |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 Hz | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 0.5 Hz | 0.00161 | 0.00040 | 0.00018 |
| 2 Hz | 0.0270 | 0.00572 | 0.00247 |
| 6.25 Hz | 0.277 | 0.0282 | 0.0104 |
| 10 Hz | 0.536 | 0.100 | 0.0195 |
| 30 Hz | 0.921 | 0.721 | 0.481 |

In the 30 Hz example, the nominal first adjacent bin receives about 92% of the centered bin's mean signal energy. This removes most mean contrast in that particular two-bin detector. The ratio gives neither the probability that the adjacent score wins nor the information left in the entire received waveform. At fixed spacing, shorter analysis looks also widen the nominal main lobe. Test joint covariance or spaced templates at unchanged elapsed time and signal energy before interpreting an isolated coherence gain as better label discrimination.

## Receiver forms worth comparing

| Form | Expected useful regime | Failure mode or cost to measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rectangular or calibrated tapered complex tone bank, energy likelihood | Known or bounded calendar, common CFO search, modest spread. Simple sufficient projection for an unknown constant complex tone amplitude | Tone leakage at residual offsets, filter-edge/taper bias, impulsive or persistent interferers, lost within-aperture channel energy |
| Shorter analysis looks within a fixed transmitted slot | Residual Doppler faster than the 160 ms whole-tone aperture. Share transforms and noise calibration among compatible modes | Segmentation adds noise degrees of freedom and widens each analysis main lobe. More captured energy alone does not imply better detection at equal search-wide false alarms |
| Small whitened subspace and weighted Gaussian covariance likelihood | Doppler modes or multiple paths described well enough to cover held-out observations | A freer model can fit both bits, a wrong key, or noise. Correlated noise and template overlaps require Gram-aware normalization, and low-rank misses need coverage evidence |
| Coherent aided scoring or conditional history fusion | Stable path, sufficient acquisition energy and trackable timing and phase | Pilot energy and availability, phase slips, self-reference bias and delayed reacquisition. Keep a calibrated noncoherent route for stale or failed guidance |
| Repeated or parity-linked tone pattern with soft symbol/vector combining | Coarse evidence can be shared when a known time/frequency constraint is valid. Code constraints help resolve erasures across distinct fades | Repeated airtime, cycle/lag ambiguity, false end and correlated outages. Assess pure repetition against a soft parity/interleaved competitor with the same elapsed time and TX budget |
| Differential or cross-ambiguity measurement | Good within-pair channel correlation with a plausible timing hypothesis. Fixed lags can use sliding products and sums | Fixed-lag autocorrelation of a repeated reference often loses absolute CFO information in magnitude. Uncertain lag/trajectory still requires a search, and noise × noise competes at low SNR |

For Gaussian subspace scoring, choose `H0=N(0,I)` after complex whitening and `H1=N(0,I+K)`, where `K` is the candidate's projected signal covariance. The log likelihood ratio is `zᴴ[I−(I+K)⁻¹]z−log det(I+K)` for proper complex observations. The determinant matters when comparing distinct mode supports. A robust statistic for impulsive or censored observations needs its own calibration. Keep label evidence, evidence of any occupied signal and evidence of physical quiet separate. A normalized score can be valid on a noise-only fixture and still confuse the bit under an off-family channel trajectory. [Matched subspace detectors](https://doi.org/10.1109/78.301849), [existing normalized-null counterexample](../../../_appraisal/highFidelity-triple_Robust_Modem/hf-normalized-null.md), [off-family acquisition evidence](../../../_appraisal/highFidelity-triple_Robust_Modem/research-reconciliation.md).

An epoch, start or tone alternative may share a physical projection with other alternatives. Keep its identity and prior when gathering that projection. The maximum over a search requires a calibrated family-wise decision. Overlapping windows, zero-padded frequency bins, and shared codewords invalidate treating every cell as independent. A union bound can set a conservative reference threshold. Measure the actual search and all coarse/fine selection on independent null episodes and wrong-schedule/interferer controls before setting an operating threshold. Never infer physical absence from a missing shortlist, an unavailable GPU result or an invalid sample interval.

## Factorization, precision and admission

Start with `f_s=3,200` complex samples/s, a 512-sample aperture and a 2,200 Hz experimental passband on signed audio offsets. A 2,048-point zero-padded DFT samples the same 160 ms aperture at 1.5625 Hz increments. The grid has 1,409 frequencies including both endpoints ±1,100 Hz before guard or filter-edge exclusions. Adjacent grid evaluations are correlated. Use consistent norm and window taper throughout. A bounded true off-grid refine, chirp-z/Bluestein, or dechirp then transform may preserve structure for some banks. A keyed permutation of existing bins changes gathers; it does not by itself create a dense unstructured template problem. An incremental transform or overlap-save filter bank needs state, error control and exact treatment of temporal halos. [cuFFT reference](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cufft/), [tcFFT author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11471), [PFB author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03579).

A direct bank for `B` observations, `N` complex samples and `F` complex templates can use `[B,2N] × [2N,2F]`. Real and imaginary projections both return. Counting multiply and add separately gives `8BNF` operations. With `N=512, F=1409`, there are 5.77 million operations per window, 0.72 GOP/s at 125 eligible starts/s for this *one* bank. A frame of 256 such outputs takes 2.89 MB at complex FP32 and fills across 2.04 s of 125/s live starts if every window comes from the same stream. Pads, drift/epoch banks, mode count and selection change the footprint. They must be counted explicitly instead of multiplying by reuse opportunities. A device-resident ring, reused templates, early exact sharing and fused reductions can reduce memory traffic. First compare against a strong FFT/channelized implementation and a direct scalar reference. Then benchmark low-bit matrix products on genuinely irregular covariance or sequence templates, with matched batches and explicit cost of packing and output materialization.

The original Matrix brief specifies signed INT4 after its declared boundary and prohibits wider numerical arithmetic. Signed four-bit operands with software multi-limb accumulation, signed-four-bit inputs with wider native accumulation, and block-scaled NVFP4 with FP32 accumulation are different contracts. NVIDIA's compute capability table lists INT4 through Ada SM 8.9 and leaves the Blackwell INT4 column blank. NVIDIA documents E2M1 NVFP4 with UE4M3 per-block scales and FP32 compute. Compiler acceptance of a type or variant, instruction family, hardware-executed operation and dynamic utilization need separate evidence. Keep strict signed arithmetic as a research branch and report what accepted accumulators and scaling would mean for other branches. The comparator in this appraisal uses FP32 inputs to cuFFT and GPU packing to E2M1 per route, outside the strict operating contract. [Original brief](../../../_appraisal/matrixModem.md), [NVIDIA current capability table](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-programming-guide/05-appendices/compute-capabilities.html), [cuBLASLt documentation](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cublas/), [projection probe](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/projection-probe/README.md).

On hardware, separately test actual INT4, feasible emulated INT4, INT8 storage/contraction, block-scaled NVFP4, FP8 and FP16 where eligible. For meaningful comparisons, freeze the data boundary after front-end filtering and Helper/DATA isolation and report signal scale, rounding/clipping, coefficient and accumulation formats and resulting null/wanted errors. Resolve end-to-end rank, likelihood margins, saturation and false completion in tests, with FP64 or exact enumeration as a bounded oracle. Numerical parity with a floating reference alone cannot qualify reception. Sparse sample probes should cover near ties, large/small amplitudes, interferers, rounding zeros, scaling of a noise-only block and mode separation. Inspect dynamic Tensor instructions with Nsight Compute or compatible hardware counters and correlate producer, packing, kernel, and readback times with Nsight Systems or runtime tracing. [Nsight Compute triage](https://docs.nvidia.com/nsight-compute/ComputeTriage/), [Nsight Systems analysis](https://docs.nvidia.com/nsight-systems/AnalysisGuide/), [CUTLASS Blackwell functionality](https://docs.nvidia.com/cutlass/latest/media/docs/cpp/blackwell_functionality.html).

Retained Robust hardware receipts show a large synthetic full Blackwell NVFP4 GEMM at 1.375 PFLOP/s and 72–75% Tensor activity, but a fine-stage-shaped control reached 11.69 TFLOP/s. A matched Robust regular-grid correlation favored cuFFT, and the separate dense FP4 coarse stage benefited when its different geometry and resident workspace supported it. Some severe earlier routes selected the wrong bit despite individual component checks. These are reasons to measure geometry, end-to-end rank and service, and they transfer as hypotheses for Matrix. [GPU and Tensor Core evidence](../../../docs/validation.md#native-throughput-and-precision-investigation), [remaining validated routes and pitfalls](../../../docs/triad-gpu-optimization.md), [precision and fallback](../../../docs/gpu-precision-controls.md).

Use one native bounded executor for sample preparation and ready score work, with `W=2H` across effective logical allocation `H`, explicit worker/owner/RAM limits and a fixed admitted lane/workspace shape per slot. Keep ordered commit with bounded out-of-order results, track sample/plan/output ownership over their lifetimes, and overlap ready leaves with earlier compute where dependencies permit. No work is credited for abandoned or unexecuted hypotheses. Admission and actual work counters should show what happened when Helper failed, when the signal remained absent, on invalid input, during reacquisition and at clock/epoch limits. Measurements of simultaneous independent streams or a large simulation batch characterize development throughput. A deployed one-stream receiver needs a separate sustained useful backlog interval, memory ceiling and cold-to-commit latency. [Development and allocation contract](../../../docs/development.md#receiver-cpu-allocation), [complete-work definition](../../../docs/robust-manual-capacity.md), [representative hardware and full-device requirements](../../../docs/triad-performance.md#representative-receiver-hardware).

## Bounded validation sequence

1. **Cheap deterministic fixtures.** Match returned tone phases/energies and band-edge normalization on both signs and lots of zero padding. Exercise fractional sample spreading, delayed tails crossing slot and filter boundaries, false quiet during invalid or censored coverage, and uncertain epoch and missing Helper separately. Check every toy-bank output against a scalar oracle, including bins outside the selected label. Calibrate own null model with fixed timing before testing full search.
2. **Conditional detector screen.** Hold 160 ms transmitted symbols and vary receiver apertures 160/80/40 ms independently of transmitted waveform. Compare known-template energy, history-assisted covariance, and short-look noncoherent combining. Covered strata start with ideal control, standard quiet, moderate and severe stationary fading, explicit offset/clock mismatch, and independent mixed-path/interference holdouts. Use matched channel and noise realization IDs. A harsher nonstationary regime must include no-service and resumption, with exact physical provenance. [Channel scenario bank](channel-envelope-review.md#scenario-bank-for-a-fair-matrix-modem-comparison).
3. **Finite-message comparison.** Once a detector handles whole-search nulls, compare no history, repeated pairs, and soft parity/interleaving with identical information payload and TX power/airtime figures. Keep a short bit/null class, a short protected message, and a sustained longer stream class. Report errors, erasures, missed acquisition, wrong bit, early false end, correct completion latency and incomplete/timeout separately. Include unchanged setup/settling, quiet guard and recurring processing. Fixed-time and fixed-energy versus equal-peak comparisons need visibly separate rows. Track occupied duty and delivered information rate.
4. **Observer and equipment checks.** Apply independent channel/noise paths and shared signal transmissions to a declared set of observer statistics, including an optimistic known-schedule control. Test independent captures by site and time, plus offset and overlap strata. Plot `Pd` at a fixed search-wide `Pfa` or required received energy across the observer bandwidth and listening settings. Include repetition with and without consistent lag and report detection separately from interference severity. [Observer basis](observer-models-review.md), [controlled figures](../../../_appraisal/algorithms-matrixModem/observer-screen/README.md).
5. **Native service and trade-off comparison.** Screen arithmetic routes with local sizing and cheap replay. Where comparable, time matched anchored workload on a full RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, report actual SKU, executed format, batches, scratch, wall time, profiler limits and cleanup. Then measure qualified active, wrong-schedule and failed/absent paths on the representative CPU/iGPU and NVIDIA devices. Count all sample prep, qualification of quiet slots, missed acquisition, admission failure, tracking and ordered delivery. A low-precision improvement qualifies only over its measured envelope.

For the first post-component study, cap work in advance to a small fixed set of paired geometry/episode strata with deliberate near-threshold and coarse failure controls. Stage 1 may stop a candidate when a deterministic example falsifies its guarantee. Stage 2 should use the predeclared replicates and interval estimator for its chosen `Pd`, `Pfa`, or difference in paired failures. Use genuinely independent channel episodes for mode variability and distinguish reuse of one trace with different noise. For example, zero independent false alarms in 3,000 opportunities gives an approximately `10⁻³` one-sided 95% upper bound only under that independence assumption. Missing precision escalates the question to a limited independent holdout or an unresolved conclusion. Nominal throughput, expensive-tail cost and workspace exhaustion are different checks, each with an explicit stopping rule. [Hierarchical test guidance](../../../docs/robust-current.md#testing-computationally-intensive-robust-changes).

Several routes could meet the objective, including cheaper exact factorizations or stronger joint likelihoods. Retain an FSK/FFT reference because it makes assumptions and omissions observable. Reconsider any preferred design when held-out evidence exposes a failure mode, especially absorption, multiple unresolved modes, failed synchronization, a mismatched null or backlog under broad search.
