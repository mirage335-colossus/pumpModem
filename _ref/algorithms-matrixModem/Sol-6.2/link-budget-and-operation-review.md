# Link budget, equipment and operating boundaries


Research snapshot, 24 November 2026. The scenarios below use the same receive reference plane for signal and noise. They give a sensitivity study for low-power HF, with receiver and observer propagation treated independently. [Channel envelope](channel-envelope-review.md), [observer models](observer-models-review.md).

## Received power and the noise reference plane

Use `C_dBm = P_TX,dBm + G_TX,dBi − L_path,dB + G_RX,dBi − losses_dB` only when the terms are independently defined. If an input called path loss already includes antenna gains, efficiencies or filter loss, apply each once. Compute `C/N₀ [dB-Hz] = C_dBm − N₀ [dBm/Hz]` at one common plane. For flat noise and a specified equivalent noise bandwidth `B_ENBW`, `SNR_dB = (C/N₀)_dB-Hz − 10 log₁₀ B_ENBW`. An occupied emission bandwidth, FFT-bin spacing, receiver ENBW, and noise figure describe different quantities. After a delay/Doppler projection or a taper, propagate the actual noise covariance or use a calibration with the identical front end and weights.

The thermal reference at 290 K is −173.98 dBm/Hz. An external antenna noise factor `F_a` expressed in dB above `kT₀` represents temperature `T_a = T₀ 10^(F_a/10)` at its stated antenna plane. Sum temperature contributions on a linear scale, with the appropriate antenna, propagation, and system reference for each term. For a passive loss with linear power gain `g` and physical temperature `T_p`, `T_out = g T_in + (1−g) T_p`. Add the receiver's input-referred equivalent noise temperature there. Under external-noise dominance and unchanged directivity/polarization, loss may attenuate wanted and external noise similarly. An inefficient antenna can still change pattern, coupling, bandwidth, local RFI pickup and receiver-noise dominance. The older appraisal's cancellation intuition is conditional on those terms. [ITU-R P.372](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-P.372), [equipment and RF effects](../../highFidelity-triple_Robust_Modem/Astra-6/50-noise-interference-and-frontends.md).

**Version the noise model.** P.372-18 is in force as of this review. Part 6 Table 1 retains the rural `(67.2, 27.7)`, quiet-rural `(53.6, 28.6)` and galactic `(52.0, 23.0)` coefficients of the historical environment curves. Evaluate `F_a = c−d log₁₀(f/MHz)` only within each curve’s applicability. The prior arithmetic uses those curves without specific time, location, antenna coupling, atmospheric noise or propagation shielding. The 2026 Ofcom result incorporated into P.372 concerns 400 MHz–1.4 GHz and indoor man-made noise. It cannot supply an HF floor correction. Year, band, receive site, bandwidth and calibration affect whether measured noise can replace a model. The 2024 Antarctic HF measurements found rural-to-residential classifications at McMurdo Station and quiet-rural noise at Black Island, 32 km away. A Dutch primary measurement study also found location effects. Those are grounds to stratify the controls, with neither a fixed ±10 dB error bar nor a universally applicable city-to-rural correction. ## What a plausible noise spread does to airtime

For illustration, assume unity combined gain at the chosen plane and three independent noise-density inputs `−144`, `−140` and `−130 dBm/Hz`. These are scenario inputs, not station predictions. A transmitter of 2 mW is +3.01 dBm and 100 mW is +20 dBm. Examples round `C/N₀` to whole dB-Hz.

| TX power | Total signal loss | Received power | `C/N₀` at −144 | at −140 | at −130 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 mW | 150 dB | −147 dBm | −3 | −7 | −17 |
| 2 mW | 165 dB | −162 dBm | −18 | −22 | −32 |
| 2 mW | 180 dB | −177 dBm | −33 | −37 | −47 |
| 100 mW | 150 dB | −130 dBm | +14 | +10 | 0 |
| 100 mW | 165 dB | −145 dBm | −1 | −5 | −15 |
| 100 mW | 180 dB | −160 dBm | −16 | −20 | −30 |

At −10 dB-Hz, an ideal 160 ms observation receives mean `E_s/N₀ = 0.016` (−17.96 dB) before taper, Doppler or timing losses. Its SNR measured in 2.2 kHz of flat noise is −43.42 dB, or −44.47 dB in 2.8 kHz. Converting a weak-signal mode's reported SNR therefore requires its stated reference bandwidth. A long code plus synchronization reserve can have very different useful throughput from `log₂ M / T`. [Waveform and repetition](waveform-and-repetition-review.md).

In coherent AWGN with ideal large time and bandwidth, a 1 KiB payload at −10 dB-Hz needs at least about 15.8 hours from `8192 ln 2 / 0.1`. Dividing 8,192 bits by the four rates in the fading-table derivation gives approximately 3.28 days, 24.3 days, 234 days and 700 days in that model. These divisions isolate the scale of the rate and take no credit for finite coding overhead, idle time, impairments or long-run stationarity. They are inappropriate as predicted file-transfer times on a real path. In the quadratic noncoherent regime, 10× higher received power can yield about 100× the asymptotic rate, so the total energy required by that model per information bit can fall about 10×. This scaling eventually leaves the quadratic regime. It also changes the observer's encounter power and the equipment's operating point. Model a power setting and a deadline together. [Information limits](information-limits-review.md).

For an RF measurement, capture calibrated gain, audio and RF filter responses, ENBW, clipping and AGC behavior, sample-clock error, absolute frequency and warm-up drift, ambient occupancy, and a noise-only interval with the same chain. Measure the actual transmit spectrum under longest dwell, worst legal tone transition, edge tones, Helper presence, and start/stop transients. Use RF attenuation, replay or a conducting fixture for bench comparisons and account for source leakage. HF daytime absorption and a weaker received noise floor can happen together, so changing only a scalar SNR cannot exercise those channel transitions. ## Operating and protocol boundaries

In the United States, Part 97 § 97.307(f)(3) sets a 2.8 kHz authorized bandwidth where that data-emission clause applies, with separate provisions for 2200 m and 630 m. Section 97.3(a)(8) defines bandwidth with a 26 dB comparison of mean signal powers. A 2.2 kHz digital tone span is useful as an experimental input for an SSB passband. RF measurement must still include pulse shaping, transitions, sidebands and any separate Helper. Check emission type and privileges under § 97.305(c), the specified/unspecified-code clauses in § 97.309, and SS authorization for a waveform classified as spread spectrum. A hop confined to an audio passband alone establishes no legal emission classification. [§ 97.307](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-D/section-97.307), [§ 97.3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-A/section-97.3), [§ 97.305](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-D/section-97.305), [§ 97.309](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-D/section-97.309), [§ 97.311](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-D/section-97.311).

Section 97.113 has restrictions on obscuring meaning and on one-way transmissions, with specified exceptions. An openly described waveform and a public zero schedule key can support a public bench profile with explicitly known parameters. Neither a public code nor spectrum width alone resolves all on-air requirements. Treat secret-schedule and confidentiality studies here as offline simulation or controlled bench results. Domain-separate any future public test sequence, confidential payload keystream and toy simulation seed. A receiver that sees a schedule match has evidence about waveform hypotheses, and cryptographic authenticity and replay resistance require a separate construction. Time/ordinal reuse and co-channel sender collisions remain open in the earlier no-overhead proposal. [§ 97.113](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-B/section-97.113), [prior stream and key analysis](../../algorithms-matrixModem/Sol-6.2/stream-keys-and-spectrum.md).

For a configured receive calendar, keep observation, soft-decode state and public physical completion distinct. An interior missing slot is an erasure if valid signal returns. Unobserved, clipped, overloaded or invalid intervals cannot count as scored absence. In an outage that makes received data statistically identical to silence, true completion and continuing transmission have the same observation distribution. No threshold can reduce both types of error on that interval when a decision is forced. The receiver can preserve an unresolved state, bound resources and report a censored outcome. File metadata, code blocks, EOF, or software quotas cannot establish successful physical completion.
