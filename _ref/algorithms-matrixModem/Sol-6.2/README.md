# Matrix Modem research

24 November 2026 · Research and experimental design

Matrix Modem is a proposed standalone C++ weak-signal modem. The first comparison should hold a simple one-way waveform fixed and ask how much wanted-signal evidence survives uncertain timing, Doppler, fading and four-bit quantization. A direct packed matrix can calculate the tone and channel-subspace projections. A factored filter bank can reuse many of the same projections. The complete comparison is useful received bits at a measured probability of correct physical completion, per transmit second and joule, with continuous receiver service and bounded memory.

## Findings that affect the first experiment

| Question | Evidence and design consequence |
| --- | --- |
| What can 2–100 mW deliver after large path loss? | At the assumed −164 dBm/Hz receive noise density and 0 dBi antennas, 2 mW with 150 dB basic loss gives about +17 dB-Hz. At 180 dB it gives −13 dB-Hz, and 100 mW at that loss gives +4 dB-Hz. Over 2.2 kHz their ideal constant-gain AWGN upper rates are about 71.7, 0.0725 and 3.62 bit/s, respectively. At the 2 mW/180 dB point, 128 information bits take at least about 29 minutes by this asymptotic ideal budgeting model, and 1 KiB about 31 hours. Actual transmission time includes acquisition, redundancy, fading, quiet completion and losses. Some HF routes never support the assumed gain or noise density. [Derivation and noise alternatives](information-limits.md). |
| Can 160 ms tones handle HF? | A 1–7 ms differential echo is short relative to the symbol and still produces frequency-selective cancellations. In the centered Gaussian single-tap model, a 160 ms correlation captures almost all mean energy at 0.5 Hz full `2σ` Doppler width, but loses about 8 dB of collected mean energy at 30 Hz. Shorter phase-coherent looks, combined with an explicitly calibrated noncoherent/partial-coherence likelihood, deserve comparison in the severe profile. The width of the pulse spectrum, alphabet spacing, search-bin spacing and physical Doppler are separate. [Regional measurements, event/outage evidence](hf-channels.md), [aperture calculation](receiver-architecture.md#timefrequency-checks-before-an-implementation-choice). |
| Does INT4 meet the accelerator requirement? | Documented NVIDIA INT4 operand paths accumulate into signed 32-bit integers. Strict four-bit primitives can represent longer sums in multiple four-bit digits, with additional work. One four-bit value per accumulator cannot represent the exact sum of general four-bit products. No device in this study has documented strict four-bit Tensor Core accumulation for this workload. Ada has documented INT4 capability. Blackwell FP4 marketing peaks and newer matrix instruction families require separate treatment. The 5650U Vega and 13900H Iris Xe/UHD classes need explicit compatible CPU or vector/shader routes, with Intel/AMD model-specific checks for newer iGPUs. [Instruction, target and throughput evidence](int4-and-accelerators.md). |
| What would GPU utilization establish? | Near-full tensor activity on heavy useful batches is a legitimate target. A single regular 2.2 kHz stream with bounded offset can leave very little useful work for a modern GPU. Redundant arithmetic can still win if it improves completed search service, deadline and evidence; neither padded operations nor low utilization on an ordinary stream alone settles the result. Compare projections, full hypothesis work and end-to-end backlog service under the actual arithmetic. Measure large independent simulation batches separately from live one-stream capacity. [Candidate workload](receiver-architecture.md#compare-complete-factorizations), [measurement plan](evaluation-plan.md). |
| Which options still merit comparison? | Start with noncoherent orthogonal/near-orthogonal FSK, with a fixed-tone control, a wider frequency-diversity version and soft erasure-aware coding. Preserve segmented correlation, a small delay–Doppler subspace, shared FFT/PFB projections and a strict native CPU baseline. Test DSSS or chirps when a specific acquisition or propagation mechanism favors them. HF field work supports a conditional DSSS/OFDM trade-off; newer OTFS-related simulations show sensitivity to estimator and geometry choices. None establishes a winner for this weak-link case. [Waveform literature and falsifiers](waveform-options.md). |
| Can an unkeyed observer detect the signal? | Even when the receiver's matched observations are much better than a wideband energy detector, known schedules, repetitive timing, Helper structure and long observations can matter. A public key gives a capable observer the same schedule. Unknown noise can help some detector models; continuous-time or multisensor access can invalidate that advantage. Report observer and intended receiver performance versus observation time at a stated false-alarm rate, with separate interference metrics. [Observer models and recent contrary results](observer-and-interference.md). |
| What is feasible for privacy and spectrum? | The requested public-zero mode provides interoperable processing with no confidentiality. Secret-code operation to obscure meaning conflicts with ordinary U.S. amateur transmissions. A one-way amateur use must fit an authorized Part 97 category, and applicable 2.8 kHz requirements are band-specific. The requested 26 dB target can be an engineering measurement kept distinct from Part 97 requirements. Existing Robust uses two XORed AES/Serpent CTR streams, while the Fast file cipher construction differs. A Matrix privacy mode needs explicit construction and counter semantics; a fixed-size ciphertext and wrong-key screening do not supply cryptographic integrity. [Regulatory sources, keyfile and streaming review](stream-keys-and-spectrum.md). |

## Where a limit is fundamental

- **Propagation outage.** If the selected path delivers indistinguishable observations for every transmitted sequence throughout the deadline, a receiver cannot recover information about the sequence. A longer code, more compute or narrower oscillators can help in other regimes. Outage frequency, persistence and alternate path availability are physical inputs to the reliability target.
- **Energy and time.** The AWGN capacity and finite-energy bounds for the stated observation model apply to a modulation or detector using that channel. They are conditional on receiver-input noise and received power. Unknown fast fading with bounded peak power can impose substantially larger costs. Some underspread-channel models predict much smaller penalties and motivate testing rather than assuming severe noncoherent loss everywhere.
- **Unrestricted observability and cryptographic reuse.** Long observations, known waveform structure and receiver-equivalent observations can admit a capable observer. A repeated deterministic stream-mask address leaks relationships between messages; a stronger cipher does not make overlapping addresses unique. No secret/shared address assumptions should be imported into public amateur-mode protection.
- **Exact scalar INT4 accumulation.** Distinct ordinary dot products map to the same narrow output under saturation or wrap. A detector can elect to use that coarse statistic and measure its separate decision performance, or retain the missing information in a multigit result. A strictly four-bit native matrix operation with this exact output is not supplied by the documented targets.

Poor GPU utilization in earlier implementations, unsuccessful predictions from a two-ray model, or a high-SNR calibration cannot rule out an entire receiver family. In the first experiment a direct matrix and a reusable bank should face the same physical waveform, calibration, complete search and commit semantics. Improve the waveform only after attributing a deficit to acquisition, coherent evidence, quantization, coding, physical availability or compute.

## Proposed first research profiles

Use a calibrated quiet/off channel, F.1487 mid-latitude moderate, severe Doppler and delay-heavy NVIS as distinct tests. Introduce measured or source-informed nonstationarity, conflicting evidence for path changes, persistent outages, impulse noise and wrong-key/collision conditions before drawing robust service conclusions. Specify region, path, season, actual delay and Doppler conventions, observation deadline and complete-message target for any proposed category. The 160 ms reference and 2.2 kHz passband start a comparison. They do not establish a final default or a tolerance percentile.

Keep these control groups explicit in future standalone appraisal fixtures.

| Control group | Candidate meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed profile | Audio passband, tone spacing, symbol duration, transition/guard, alphabet/coding, shift and allowed band, optional FHSS and wrong-key admission. The nominal manually rounded shift can domain-separate the key schedule while the protocol keeps hop frequencies out of that derivation. |
| Clock and hardware | RF/baseband and audio oscillators, epoch and time/frequency uncertainty, measured device response and device latency; separate actual Live/Duplex devices from simulator conditions. A GPSDO can reduce oscillator uncertainty and does not freeze an ionospheric path. |
| Optional Helper | Independently estimated timing/frequency/clock corrections with uncertainty, explicit power/split and callsign/identification behavior; compare equal total energy with Helper off and on. Never count Helper confidence as DATA evidence. |
| Simulation | Transmit power/path loss or calibrated target `C/N₀`, applied channel gain, correlated delay/Doppler and interference, transmitter noise, frequency-selective amplitude dynamics, region/time and frontend errors. Maintain explicit Off controls and derived units. |
| Keys and content | Shared keyfile format and source entropy assessment, public scheme vs secret mode where separately authorized, exact per-stream address and collision handling, bounded opaque input/export, and physical quiet completion. The configured FEC geometry cannot be set by a received length. |

This is a broad targeted literature review, with primary studies, official standards and architecture references, explicit assumptions or access limits, and reconciliation against the repository's prior evidence. It does not establish representative HF availability for an unselected route, an exact achievable bitrate, sensitivity at a field reliability target or actual GPU throughput. No Matrix Modem was implemented in this research pass. The first candidate is deliberately simple enough to be falsifiable. Better receivers or waveforms may emerge when compared at the same energy, deadline, physical conditions and complete receiver work.
