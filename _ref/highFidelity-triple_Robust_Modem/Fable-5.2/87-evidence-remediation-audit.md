were locators, not 173 independent scientific questions; the original word count

are preserved, with feasible local steps separated from truly external inputs.

Locators in family inventories refer to headings and, where supplied, lines at
the audited baseline; lines move after editing. Short chapter numbers refer to
the [reading map](README.md#reading-map). Historical source lists are traced to
07bcbc5d unless another revision is stated. References describe actual inspected
passages, not proof that every page of a long report was reviewed. Planned
retrieval routes are not claims of newly verified live availability.

Priority: **P0** misleading status or an evidence dependency requiring repair
before interpretation; **P1** consequential research/selected-scenario work;
**P2** material alternative or coverage/transfer question; **P3** context only.
Types: **R** source review, **D** existing-data analysis, **A** calculations,
**S** physical/statistical specification, **E** new/external evidence, **I** later
implementation/qualification. A multitype row is not wholly blocked because one
step is external. Source-access closure, mathematical-control closure and natural
calibration are never interchangeable.

Rows can share sources and dependencies. The family inventory is not a set of
independent experiments or an additive cost estimate. Use WP00 to deduplicate
source reads and propagate corrections. A contextual lead may be disposed as
unused only after checking that no consequential claim depends on it; this
cannot silently remove a requested physical domain.

## Earth–Moon–Earth

### Current EME status

Chapters 40 and 41 now have a per-source disposition for their original missing
lists, current query receipts, and substantial literature synthesis. Chapter 84
pins a specific 5.760 GHz mathematical fixture: DE440-compatible geometric
two-leg retarded paths, orientation kernels, station coordinates, beam
patterns, transmit power, four families of angular/cross-section kernels,
polarization alternatives, clock/noise controls, an outage comparator, and a
costly finite lunar-surface quadrature. Independent quadrature and scalar
checks are reported in 84 and 86. These are useful completed research outputs.

They do not establish a measured 5–6 cm whole-disc scattering function, total
cross section, polarization distribution, stochastic time history, calibrated
site temperature, or complete two-station complex echo. The 433.8 MHz data are
a different self-echo experiment and have not been decoded/analysed from IQ.
Mini-RF products are local Earth-to-orbit SAR and do not directly measure the
Earth–Moon–Earth channel. The scenario has not acquired the declared SPICE
kernels, run its ephemeris, implemented its channel, or tested the native modem.
Keep those statuses distinct.
