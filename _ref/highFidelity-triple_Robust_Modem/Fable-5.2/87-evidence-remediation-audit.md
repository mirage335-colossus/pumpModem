## Reading the dispositions

A completed research action can establish a result, reject a proposed
interpretation, identify an underdetermined observable, or demonstrate a failed
numerical check. It does not automatically establish a calibrated physical
model. Read the result and boundary columns together.

- **R**: primary-source review or a specified, actually encountered access limit.
- **D**: analysis of an existing public product, with observable/selection limits.
- **A**: calculation or numerical discriminator; pass/failure is stated locally.
- **S**: reproducible specification and source-to-scenario mapping.
- **E**: missing external calibration, operation record or physical observation.
- **I**: later native implementation, generator/receiver test or hardware qualification.

An E/I boundary does not excuse an unperformed accessible R/D/A action. The
current rows reflect actual follow-through before that boundary was assigned.
Unread contextual sources remain individually named in their subject chapters;
their uninspected numbers do not become selected inputs. A source's publication
type, full-text access, reviewed passage and physical applicability are separate
attributes. No claim is made that every historical sentence or every publication
in the field has been independently verified.

## Completion and update rule

The final review and documentation-check receipt are in
[80](80-completion-plan.md#5-reproducibility-retention-and-review).
These dispositions do not turn failed checks, mismatched observables or missing
physical calibration into passes. They also do not require endless recovery of
unused contextual bibliography when inspected primary sources already support
the selected claim and the contrary mechanism remains visible.

Reopen the affected row when a new source, producer dictionary, corrected
reference implementation or matched measurement changes its conclusion. Follow
its source-to-scenario consumers through the owning chapters before updating a
preset or receiver claim. Later empirical and native qualification must retain
their original failure conditions, uncertainty, observation denominator and
resource limits; research closure supplies no automatic pass for those stages.
