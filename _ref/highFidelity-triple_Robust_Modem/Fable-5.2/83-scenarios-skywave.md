- **Status.** Research specification, not an implemented simulator or modem result. Values retain the source's path, frequency, estimator, detection condition and access limits. “Adverse” means a useful conditional challenge, not a frequency-of-occurrence claim. An outage is a real branch, not a finite worst-case channel.

## Common signal and channel contract

Use a common physical state to drive the Helper and every DATA carrier at its actual frequency. For carrier frequency `f`, the propagation response is represented as

`h_f(t, τ) = Σ_m Σ_{q∈{O,X}} sqrt(P_{m,q,f}(t)) · z_{m,q,f}(t) · g_{m,q,f}(t, τ − τ_{m,q,f}(t)) · exp(j φ_{m,q,f}(t))`,
where `φ(t) = φ(0) + 2π ∫₀ᵗ ν(u) du`.

Here `m` is a geometric mode (for example 1F, 2F, E, mixed, or a measured diffuse component); `q` is an O or X magneto-ionic component; `P` is mean received power; `z` supplies small-scale complex fading with the declared Doppler spectrum; `g` is the normalized within-mode delay profile; `τ` is group delay; and `ν` is Doppler centroid. Integrate a changing centroid to preserve the intended instantaneous frequency, and do not add Doppler already generated by a moving propagation delay a second time. A source without a parameter leaves it unassigned or exposes a named sensitivity input; it does not justify filling a joint distribution by guesswork. The family of frequency responses must describe one physical linear operator acting on the complete composite emission, with one shared time-varying ionosphere and route state; separate subband computation is valid only when it reproduces that operator and its cross-frequency correlations.

For reproducible synthetic controls, the F.1487 two-ray setup is exactly two equal-power, independent Rayleigh paths, differential delay and per-tap Gaussian Doppler spectrum with stated `2σ`. It is a *test comparator*, not a fit to all natural links. For a source-matched replay, store measured power, delay, centroid and spectrum with the source's detection threshold, observation window, sounder resolution and censored/missing intervals. Report conditional channel metrics separately from availability: silence/undetected epochs remain in the availability denominator. Distinguish ionospheric absence from receiver acquisition failure, decoder failure and packet loss.

O/X dependence is evidenced only for the measured single-frequency NVIS links below. It must not be copied as a frequency-correlation model for Helper and DATA or between DATA carriers. Cross-frequency coherence, especially at the modem's 550 Hz Helper pilots and configured carrier separations, remains unresolved. For sensitivity analysis only, a user may declare alternative coupling bounds across carriers; label these as mathematical bounds, not natural channel distributions.

## Minimal evaluation matrix

For each selected scenario, render the unchanged transmitted Helper and DATA streams through the same channel state. Record (1) source inputs and provenance, (2) requested versus available propagation modes, (3) per-carrier received power/C/N0 and censoring, (4) channel impulse response and Doppler observables with estimator, (5) Helper acquisition outcome, (6) DATA tracking/bit/null decisions, and (7) physical quiet-slot completion. Include a continuous baseline, at least one measurement-backed adverse case, a real outage/transition case, and one explicit model-disagreement comparison (two-ray stationary Watterson versus synthetic realizations matched to measured unequal/multimode summaries). Keep numerical-severity comparisons conditional on matched input waveform, noise, receiver settings and availability state.

This matrix specifies evidence coverage; it is not a claim that all strata have qualified modem performance. Unknown-start waveform/ordinal acquisition, population outage probabilities, joint carrier covariance, and communication success probabilities remain unqualified until measured or separately justified.
